**英文原文**

The Mantolith was a large assault drill used to deliver troops in subterranean assaults during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. These vehicles were large enough to allow for teleportation chambers, allowing them to deliver troops to its target without breaching the surface. Several Mantolith's were used by the Sons of Horus during the assault on the Saturnine Gate in the Siege of Terra.

**校订译文**

地幔岩型是一种大型突击钻，用于在大远征及荷鲁斯之乱期间通过地下突击输送部队。它的内部空间足够容纳传送室，因此无需钻出地表，就能将战士送往目标位置。泰拉围城期间，荷鲁斯之子在进攻土星之门时曾使用数台地幔岩型突击钻。
