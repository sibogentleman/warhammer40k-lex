**英文原文**

The Grav-Rhino was a rare and advanced variant of the Rhino armoured personnel carrier equipped with Anti-Gravity drives, allowing it to hover over the earth itself. This vehicle is known to have been used almost exclusively by the Adeptus Custodes and Sisters of Silence during the Horus Heresy.
