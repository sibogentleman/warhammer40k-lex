**英文原文**

It belongs to the Salamanders Chapter, and was supposedly used to transport Vulkan himself into battle on many occasions. Such is its holiness that Nocturne's Hammer has granted its own personal Techpriest, and always the most experienced crew available. Although accounts of its actions are rare, it is known to have taken part in the Siege of Devlin's Fastness. Due to its immense importance to the chapter, Nocturne's Hammer now resides within the Salamander's reliquary on Prometheus. Each new century, the Master of the Forge strikes the rune of activation on Nocturne's Hammer. It is taken as a bad sign if the engine does not take off the first time it is activated.

**校订译文**

它属于火蜥蜴战团，据说曾多次载着沃坎本人奔赴战场。由于地位极其神圣，夜曲星之锤号有专属技术祭司照料，并总是配备资历最深的车组。有关其战绩的记录虽不多，但已知它参加过德夫林要塞围城战。如今，鉴于它对战团的重大意义，这辆犀牛被珍藏于普罗米修斯的火蜥蜴圣物库。每逢新世纪开始，铸造大师便会按下其启动符文；若引擎未能一次启动，就会被视为凶兆。

**校注：** reliquary 此处为容纳车辆的圣物库，不是小型圣物箱。
