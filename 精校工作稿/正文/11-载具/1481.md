**英文原文**

The Admonisher is a heavily armoured personnel carrier, used by the Adeptus Arbites and Imperial prison wardens. Admonishers are a variant of the Rhino, open-topped to allow the wardens it carries to maintain overwatch on the convicts they herd. A huge, V-shaped man catcher is mounted at the tank’s front like the prow of a warship.
