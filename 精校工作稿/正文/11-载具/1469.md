## Overview 概述

<!-- A1469-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

The data used to redevelop the Iron Fist was based on STC fragments recovered from the forge world Porphetus before that world was lost to a tyranid invasion. Some Adeptus Mechanicus military commanders were making use of the Iron Fist in late M41 despite the vehicle not yet being sanctioned for widespread deployment.

**校订译文**

铁拳的重新研制依据一批 STC 残片展开。这些残片出土于波菲图斯铸造世界，在该世界遭泰伦入侵、沦陷之前便已取回。尽管该型尚未获准大规模部署，部分机械修会军事指挥官在第41千年末期已经开始使用。

<!-- A1469-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

The Iron Fist is based on a modified, open-topped variant of the Rhino chassis. Its primary armament is a quad-heavy bolter battery. In addition, the vehicle was equipped with refractor generators, rendering it near-immune to small arms fire.
