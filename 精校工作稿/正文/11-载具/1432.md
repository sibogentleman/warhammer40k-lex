**英文原文**

Hammerfall Bunkers are dropped from orbit to provide a beachhead, secure a landing zone, area defence, or to simply wreak havoc behind enemy lines. Crewed by hard-wired Servitors with area denial programming, their main weapon is a Hammerfall Missile Launcher. Meanwhile the bunker's side guns can be either heavy bolters or heavy flamers.

落锤堡可从轨道空投，用于建立登陆据点、确保着陆区安全、执行区域防御，或在敌后制造破坏。它由以硬线接入、装有区域封锁程序的机仆操控，主武器是落锤导弹发射器，侧面武器则可选用重型爆矢枪或重型火焰喷射器。
