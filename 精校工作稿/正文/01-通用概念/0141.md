**英文原文**

The Collegiate Extremis is a powerful Adeptus Mechanicus organization that ensures Forge Worlds obey the laws of the Lore Mechanicus. Members of the Lords Dragon can offer roles in the Collegiate Extremis to those they see as suited for such a dangerous vocation. They ruthlessly investigate and pursue those suspected of tech heresy.

## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0141-B0003 -->

<!-- A0141-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

In function, they are similar to the Adeptus Arbites who enforce the Lex Imperialis. However, their duties cross over more into the realm of the Mechanicus's day-to-day, than the Arbites are in the Imperium's. This leads the Collegiate more likely to be involved in criminal investigations that the Arbites would consider to be beneath their notice. The Collegiate's higher ranks are concerned with great crimes against the Lore, while the lower ranked Procurators are assigned to a single world. This also includes Imperial worlds which only contain Mechanicus enclaves. In such instances, these Procurators could also serve as liaisons between the Mechanicus's Astynomia Enforcers and non-Mechanicus organizations. The Collegiate Extremis's duties can lead to conflict with the Astynomia, though, and there are long-standing suspicions between the two organizations. However, the Collegiate does recruit members of the Astynomia, who have excelled within its ranks.

<!-- /A0141-B0004 -->

<!-- A0141-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在功能上，他们类似于执行帝国律法的法务部。然而，他们的职责比帝国中的法务部更深入机械教的日常生活。这导致学院更有可能参与法务部认为不值得他们注意的刑事调查。学院的高层负责处理针对知识的重大罪行，而较低级别的检察官则被分配到一个单一的世界。这也包括只包含机械教飞地的帝国世界。在这种情况下，这些检察官也可以作为机械教的律法督查和非机械教组织之间的联络人。然而，绝境学院的职责可能会导致与律法督查的冲突，这两个组织之间长期存在猜疑。然而，学院确实招募了在其队伍中表现出色的律法督查成员。

</details>

**校订译文**

绝境学院的职能类似于执行帝国律法的法务部，但更深入机械修会的日常事务，因此也会调查法务部通常不屑过问的普通犯罪。学院高层处理严重违反机械律法的案件，较低阶的检察官则分驻单个世界，包括仅设有机械修会飞地的帝国行星。在后一种情况下，他们也可担任律法督查与外部机构之间的联络人。职权重叠使学院与律法督查时有冲突，两者长期互相猜疑；但学院仍会从律法督查中吸收表现出色的人才。
