**英文原文**

The Prefecture Magisterium (also known as the Malagra) is a division of the Adeptus Mechanicus, acting as its de facto secret police. It is charged with preserving the dogma of the Cult Mechanicus and the persecution of Hereteks. It operates military Cohorts and hunter-killer clades which are maintained on Forge Worlds.

**英文原文**

Agents of the Prefecture Magisterium are known as Arcuitors and are usually Magi themselves. Known for their devoutness to the Cult Mechanicus, these sinister figures are also dubbed the Paternis Malagra or Fathers of the Malagra, for they are often ancient beyond belief and heavily augmented. The Arcuitors do not officially lead the Prefecture Magisterium, but through their actions are the agendas of the organization shaped. A senior Arcuitor can call upon great resources against those they deem worthy of persecution such as the Ossifram Majority or the Dark Mechanicum.

辖区裁判所的特工被称为操肢者，通常是圣贤士。这些险恶的人物以对机械教派的虔诚而闻名，也被称为马拉格拉父辈或马拉格拉之父，因为他们往往是古老得令人难以置信的，并且被大量强化。操肢者并没有正式领导辖区裁判所，但通过他们的行动，该组织的议程得以形成。高级操肢者可以调用大量资源来对抗他们认为值得迫害的人，如骨骼架构派或黑暗机械教。

**校订译文**

裁判所特工称为操肢者，通常自身也是贤者。他们对机械教派极为虔诚，年龄往往古老得难以置信，且接受大量机械强化，因此又称 Paternis Malagra，即“马拉格拉之父”。他们并非组织名义上的领袖，却以实际行动塑造其方向。资深操肢者能够调用大量资源，打击骨骼架构派、黑暗机械教等被其认定应受迫害的对象。
