**英文原文**

The smallest unit of a Space Marine force is typically the squad, composed of ten marines, however, it is possible for a ten-man squad to be split into two five-man units called combat squads. When a unit is split into combat squads, four of the marines fall under the command of the Sergeant while the remaining four marines are led by the squad leader.

**校订译文**

星际战士部队最小的常规单位通常是十人小队，但也可拆为两个五人战斗分队。拆分后，军士带领四名战士，小队领队带领另外四名战士；计入各自指挥者后，每个分队均为五人。

**校注：** 正文 combat squad 暂统一战斗分队，与十人 squad 小队区分；英文篇名 Battle Squad 与正文术语不完全相同，原英文保留。
