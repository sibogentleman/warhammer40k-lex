**英文原文**

Narcotics are addictive, illegal substances used for their pleasurable side-effects, as opposed to combat drugs and stimms which are used to strengthen the body and mind for battle. Narcotics can be ingested in a variety of forms, most notably inhaled by smoking or injected directly into the bloodstream. Some narcotics share both methods, or take on other forms of use entirely. Smokable forms are commonly kept in paper cylinders, much like a common cigarette, and are ingested using an igniter and a form of filter. Injected versions are held in injector vials or other forms of containment. Narcotics are known to take the form of rocks, glass, plants, gases, and liquids.

<!-- A0050-B0002 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

麻醉药物是一种令人上瘾的非法物质，因其令人愉快的副作用而被使用，与之相对的是用于增强身心战斗力的战斗药物和兴奋剂。麻醉药物可以以多种形式摄入，最明显的是通过吸入或直接注射到血液中。一些麻醉药物共享这两种方法，或者完全采用其他形式的使用。可吸入的形式通常保存在纸筒中，就像普通的香烟一样，使用点火器和某种形式的过滤器摄入。注射版本保存在注射器小瓶或其他形式的容器中。众所周知，麻醉药物的形式有块、晶、植物、气体和液体。

</details>

**校订译文**

本篇所称麻醉药物，是为追求愉悦感而使用、具有成瘾性的违禁物质，与为了作战而强化身心的战斗药物及兴奋剂相对。它们有多种摄入方式，常见的是吸入烟雾或直接注入血液；有些两种方式都可使用，另一些则另有用法。供吸食的制品常像香烟一样卷成纸筒，点燃后经滤嘴吸入；注射用制品则储存在注射药瓶或其他容器中。这些物质可能呈块状、玻璃状，也可能是植物、气体或液体。

**校注：** 本篇开头把 narcotics 定义为非法消遣药物，但后续清单也包含合法医疗药、战斗兴奋剂和虚构毒物；分类并不严密，本稿保留条目组织方式，不把它当作现实药理分类。

## History 历史

<!-- /A0050-B0003 -->

<!-- A0050-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

Since the dawn of time, humanity has used narcotics to alter their physical or psychological state; a truth that has persisted into the 41st Millennium. The most common mind-altering substance is lho-leaf. Easy to obtain, the stick version (which is lit and inhaled via filters) is popular among the Imperium citizenry and military (in the case of the Imperial Guard and the various PDFs). Obscura is another widely used narcotic, illegal, and commonly used by the middle and upper-class citizens of hives in obscura dens. The enforcement of drug control falls upon planetary authorities, though Inquisition can become involved if it becomes a widespread problem.
