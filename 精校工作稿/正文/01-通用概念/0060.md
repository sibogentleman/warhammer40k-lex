**英文原文**

Ceramite is a form of ceramo-metallic hyper-alloy partly containing crystals, used in higher-end types of Imperial armour such as carapace armour, power armour and Tactical Dreadnought Armour, as well as in the armour of Knight suits and Space Marine vehicles. Ceramite conducts almost no heat, making it especially heat-resistant and effective against energy-based weapons.

**英文原文**

Ceramite is a kind of bonded ceramic armour, especially tough, but light. Repairs are made by setting new layers of ceramite over existing ones. Unlike metal, Ceramite doesn’t rupture or dent on impact, but rather shatters, and it takes a projectile of considerable power and high calibre to break through it. Cracked pieces of ceramite armour can be held together with miliary-grade fusion tape if repairs are not possible. Damaged Ceramite has been described as a dull, leaden grain.

**校订译文**

陶钢是一种结合陶瓷的装甲材料，坚韧而轻巧。维修时可在原有表面覆上新的陶钢层。受到撞击后，它不像金属那样撕裂或凹陷，而是碎裂，但必须遭到威力可观的大口径弹丸命中才会被击穿。若暂时无法修复，裂开的甲片可用军用熔合胶带固定。破损陶钢的断面被形容为暗淡、铅灰色的颗粒状质地。
