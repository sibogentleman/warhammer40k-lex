**英文原文**

The Lex Imperialis, also called the Dictates Imperialis, is the legal code of the Imperium. Claimed to be the word of the Emperor himself, it is a vast body of laws and codes compiled over ten thousand years that Judges of the Adeptus Arbites spend their entire lives studying and compiling in the Book of Judgement. Though each world of the Imperium has its own unique set of rules, the Lex Imperialis is the definitive and absolute word, ruling above all others. The Lex Imperialis is ruthlessly enforced by the Adeptus Arbites.

**校订译文**

帝国律法，又称帝国法令，是帝国的法律体系，被宣称为帝皇本人的意旨。它由一万多年间积累的庞大法律与规章构成，法务部法官穷尽一生研读、整理，将之编入《审判之书》。帝国各世界虽有各自的地方规则，帝国律法仍是凌驾其上的最终权威，由法务部毫不留情地执行。

**英文原文**

While the Dicatates Imperialis are extensive, the huge volume of prior cases and sometimes contradictory rulings can make it difficult to determine the correct decision. In especially complex cases, it may take centuries to reach an outcome. A Judge may spend his entire life deliberating, scrutinizing, and trying to fathom out the issues, only to pass his work on, unfinished, for others to continue. Millennia later, though the accused are long dead, a ruling is finally made and justice must be meted out upon the distant descendants and those obscurely associated with the original transgressor.

帝国法令内容浩繁，历代判例数量庞大，裁决有时还彼此矛盾，因此很难确定正确判决。格外复杂的案件可能数百年都没有结果。一位法官即便毕生斟酌、审查，仍可能只能把未完成的工作交给继任者。数千年后，判决终于作出，被告却早已死去，于是所谓“正义”便施加于其遥远后裔，以及那些与最初违法者仅有隐约联系的人身上。
