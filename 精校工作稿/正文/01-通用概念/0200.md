**英文原文**

These units first appeared only when Horus openly began his rebellion and were ad-hoc assemblies of veteran warriors intended to counter the Daemonic allies of the traitors. Though initially wielding hastily manufactured weaponry and poorly understood superstition in their brave attempts to stem the Chaos tide, by the end of the conflict they were a highly disciplined arm of the Legiones Astartes and countered the threat of the foulest Xenos as well as the Daemonic.

<!-- /A0200-B0003 -->

荷鲁斯公开反叛后，这类部队才首次出现，由老兵临时编组，专门对抗叛徒的恶魔盟友。最初，他们只能依靠仓促制造的武器与尚未充分理解的驱邪仪式，勇敢地抵挡混沌浪潮。到战争结束时，他们已成为阿斯塔特军团中纪律严明的专业力量，既抗击恶魔，也应对最可憎的异形威胁。
