**英文原文**

A Forge Temple, also known as a Forge, is a place used by the Adeptus Mechanicus for research and construction of technologies. A Forge Temple can range in size from a single building to an entire city. The senior Tech-priest at a Forge Temple holds the rank of Forge Master.

**英文原文**

Even a minor Forge Temple holds a great amount of data in its archive towers, kept at temperature by wall-mounted atomisers spewing cooling incenses. If necessary, this amount of data can be transferred elsewhere, taking at least ten hours to complete for the last few thousands of zettabytes. Only a skeleton crew is necessary to successfully complete the data transfer.
