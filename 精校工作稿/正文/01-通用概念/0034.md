**英文原文**

Enuncia is an ancient language, but instead of communication, the language was used as a tool or a weapon: when spoken aloud or written, Enuncia is capable of manipulating the very fabric of reality, a power beyond even the most potent psykers. It is incredibly dangerous, especially since it required no natural psyker ability, and could potentially be used by anyone.

## History and emergence 历史和出现

<!-- /A0034-B0003 -->

<!-- A0034-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

Enuncia is very obscure, with only a few fragmentary references appearing anywhere in Imperial records. Even fewer of these references include any information on the use of Enuncia. One theory states that Enuncia accounts for ancient human legends concerning the use of "magic"; another says that it was one of the tools of creation itself. In Terra's ancient past the Cognitae attempted to build a tower etched in Enuncia, but were foiled by the forces of the Emperor and Ollanius Persson.

<!-- /A0034-B0004 -->

<!-- A0034-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

暗言非常晦涩难懂，帝国记录中只出现了少数零碎的参考文献。这些参考文献中包含有关暗言使用的任何信息的更少。一种理论认为，暗言解释了古代人类关于使用“魔法”的传说；另一种说，它本身就是创造的工具之一。在古代泰拉，闻道学派试图用暗言建造一座塔，但被帝皇和欧兰尼奥斯·佩松的部队挫败。

</details>

**校订译文**

关于暗言的知识极为隐秘，帝国档案中仅有少量零散记载，其中提及具体用法的更是寥寥无几。一种理论认为，古代人类有关“魔法”的传说源自暗言；另一种理论则认为，暗言本就是创造万物的工具之一。在远古泰拉，闻道学派曾试图建造一座刻满暗言的高塔，但遭到帝皇与欧良尼乌斯·佩松率军阻止。

<!-- /A0034-B0005 -->

<!-- A0034-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

The few effects of Enuncia observed can include the control of daemons (without the usual bargaining), or as a potent weapon against enemies, causing physical damage to the target ranging from uncontrollable vomiting to cranial rupturing. However, the reality-warping effects can also damage the speaker, many of whom suffer wounds to their mouths and throats when speaking the words. Uniquely, neverborn are as suseptible to the effects of Enuncia as their counterparts in the materium. Likewise, machines attempting to process written forms of the language can suffer spontaneous breakdowns.

<!-- /A0034-B0006 -->

<!-- A0034-B0007 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

观察到的暗言的少数影响可能包括控制恶魔（无需通常的讨价还价），或作为对抗敌人的有力武器，对目标造成身体伤害，从无法控制的呕吐到颅骨破裂。然而，现实扭曲效应也会损害发言者，其中许多人在发言时口腔和喉咙会受伤。独特的是，无生者和物质界中的同类一样容易受到暗言的影响。同样，试图处理书面语言形式的机器也可能发生自发故障。

</details>

**校订译文**

已观察到的暗言效果，包括不经通常的交易便能控制恶魔，以及作为武器伤害敌人，轻则令目标呕吐不止，重则使其头颅爆裂。但这种扭曲现实的力量也会反噬说话者，许多人念出暗言时会伤及口腔和喉咙。特别的是，无生者与物质界的生物同样容易受到暗言影响。试图处理暗言书面形式的机器，也可能突然故障。

**英文原文**

Two known cases could be extracted from the archives concerning the Horus Heresy. In the first, use of a single Enuncia "word" is attributed to the Space Wolves Skjald Kasper Hawser. He successfully employed the word during the Battle of Prospero, to fight against a Tzeentchian Daemon which revealed itself to be the entity Russ thought used Hawser's body to spy on the Space Wolves. Hawser, although mangling the daemon, suffered injuries to his throat and teeth after employing such a word of power.

**英文原文**

However, Molotch survived, and, after allying with a fellow Cognitae "graduate," Jader Trice, approached the problem from a different angle. He attempted to reconstruct Enuncia from scratch, using an enormous array of logic engines and cogitators to combine letters and phonemes in all possible combinations. To do this, he set himself up as Lord Governor Subsector on Eustis Majoris, using the resources of the Administratum, and several warp-tainted data engines recovered from the lost Mergent Worlds, which had been embroiled in a warp storm for centuries. The project, controlled by a group called the Secretists, gradually extracted fragments of Enuncia from seemingly random data.
