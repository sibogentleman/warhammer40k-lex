**英文原文**

Paladins, known as the pure-blades, were the elite swordsmen, guardians of the secrets of the Hekatonystika, and Company Champions of the the Dark Angels Legion. The Ninth Order boasted at least fifty paladins during the Great Crusade who acted as the honour guard of the Primarch Lion El'Jonson. They carried powered longblades and blast-shields.

**校订译文**

圣骑士又称“纯粹之刃”，是黑暗天使军团的精锐剑士、百秘骑士团秘密的守护者，也是各连队的冠军。大远征期间，第九骑士团至少有五十名圣骑士，担任原体莱昂·艾尔’庄森的荣誉卫队。他们装备动力长刃与防爆盾。
