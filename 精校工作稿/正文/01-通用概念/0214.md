**英文原文**

The Primarchs were the twenty genetically-engineered "sons" of the Emperor of Mankind, and the genetic "fathers" of the Space Marine Legions. The Emperor used his own DNA in their creation, and they were designed to be far superior to the average human: immensely larger, stronger, hardier, faster, and more intelligent. They were also incredibly charismatic, as their main role was to be generals and leaders of the Imperial military. Each had their own purpose and specialty, allowing them to serve in specific functions that the Emperor's forces would need in reconquering the galaxy.

## Creation of the Primarchs 原体的诞生

<!-- /A0214-B0007 -->

<!-- A0214-B0008 -->
**英文原文**

The Primarchs were created in a secret underground laboratory on Terra under the tightest security. According to Ingethel the Ascended, the Primarchs were created by the Emperor with the aid of forbidden sciences and arcane lore derived from the Warp. Eldrad Ulthran also states that the Emperor utilized dark powers to construct the Primarchs. It is speculated the Primarchs were imbued with energies and knowledge that the Emperor gained in a Warp Portal on Molech. The genetic material of the powerful Perpetual Erda were used in the creation of the Primarchs and she viewed them as her "sons". Perturabo would later estimate his own birth to be 792.M30, perhaps giving a date for the Primarch's creation.

原体是在泰拉的一个秘密地下实验室里，在最严密的安全措施下诞生的。根据升天者英格瑟尔的说法，原体是由帝皇在来自亚空间的禁忌科学和神秘学识的帮助下创造的。艾尔德拉德·乌索然还指出，帝皇利用黑暗力量创造了原体。据推测，原体们充满了帝皇在摩洛的亚空间门户中获得的能量和知识。强大的永生者尔达的遗传物质被用于创造原体，她将他们视为自己的“儿子”。佩图拉博后来估计他自己的出生日期是792-M30，也许给出了原体创造的日期。

</details>

**校订译文**

原体在泰拉一处严密戒备的地下实验室中诞生。据升天者英格泰尔称，帝皇借助了源自亚空间的禁忌科学与秘术；艾尔德拉德·乌瑟兰也说，帝皇曾动用黑暗力量。有人据此推测，原体体内灌注了帝皇进入摩洛亚空间门户后取得的力量与知识。强大的永生者尔达也提供了遗传物质，并将原体视为自己的儿子。佩图拉博后来估计自己生于 792.M30，这或许为原体的创造年代提供了线索。

**英文原文**

However, the Gods of Chaos somehow managed to spirit them away just prior to their maturation with a great Warp vortex and it is speculated that they also managed to tamper with the infant Primarchs. The scattering of the Primarchs may have been the result of a Causal Loop created when Argel Tal and his men were sent back in time inside the Eye of Terror to the Emperor's laboratory, as they were convinced by Ingethel the Ascended to destroy the Gellar Fields protecting the infants from the touch of the Chaos Gods. Almost immediately after the Gellar Field generators were disabled, the Primarchs were sucked into a Warp vortex. Other sources state that the Perpetual Erda was involved in the theft of the Primarchs and created the Warp Vortex that scattered them due to her anger over the fate the Emperor planned for her "children". According to Erebus, Erda was unwittingly manipulated by the Chaos Gods into committing this act. Several Primarchs, including Konrad Curze, Roboute Guilliman, and even Sanguinius all came to believe that the scattering of the Primarchs and their upbringing on distant worlds had all been engineered by the Emperor.

**英文原文**

Scattered across the galaxy, beyond the Emperor's reach, and all landed on different worlds vastly separated from their Father. The Emperor could sense that the infant Primarchs were alive, but was unable to reach them. Therefore he used their previously collected DNA samples to create the Space Marines. The genetic material of each one of the Primarchs was used to found a single Space Marine Legion, twenty in total. The new homeworlds of the infant Primarchs and subsequent experiences on those worlds would influence and mold them to a large extent. There they quickly grew to adulthood and often rose to power, ruling, leading, and helping the inhabitants. As the Emperor found them, he was generally pleased by their actions, and quickly gave them the leadership of their respective legion.

## Rediscovery 回归

<!-- /A0214-B0018 -->

<!-- A0214-B0019 -->
**英文原文**

Traditional accounts state that the first Primarch to be found was Horus, who became the Emperor's favourite. However a dubious account given by Alpharius states he was the first, and landed on Terra itself following the scattering. Most Primarchs somehow recognized the Emperor on sight, and immediately pledged their allegiance on the spot. A few, (Leman Russ, Ferrus Manus and Vulkan) only followed him after being bested in contests. Angron refused, and was abducted from his homeworld, something he never really accepted. The last to be found was Alpharius.

<!-- /A0214-B0019 -->

<!-- A0214-B0020 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

根据传统说法，第一位被发现的原体是荷鲁斯，他成为了帝皇的宠儿。然而，阿尔法瑞斯给出的一个可疑的说法是，他是第一个，在分散之后就降落在了泰拉。大多数原体一看到帝皇就认出了他，并立即当场宣誓效忠。一些人（黎曼·鲁斯、费鲁斯·马努斯和沃坎）在比赛中被击败后才追随他。安格隆拒绝了，并被从他的家园世界绑架，这是他从未真正接受的原因。最后被发现的是阿尔法瑞斯。

</details>

**校订译文**

通常的记载称，荷鲁斯最先被寻回，成为帝皇最宠爱的儿子。但阿尔法瑞斯在一份真伪难辨的自述中声称，自己才是第一个，失散后便落在泰拉。多数原体初见帝皇就认出了他，当场效忠；黎曼·鲁斯、费鲁斯·马努斯和沃坎等，则在较量落败后才承认他的领导。安格隆拒绝服从，被强行带离故乡，此事令他始终无法释怀。按公开记载，最后被寻回的是阿尔法瑞斯。

格雷戈·艾森霍恩提及了白色执政官星际战士战团的“原体”，他表示，他特意赞扬了一位英勇的军士，在色雷斯主星，这位军士以牺牲自己的生命为代价救了他的命。由于白色执政官是一个子战团，而且帝国内没有存活或活跃的原体，他似乎将“原体”一词与战团长互换使用。

**校订译文**

格雷戈·艾森霍恩讲述色雷斯主星上的经历时说，一位英勇军士牺牲自己救下了他，他因此专门向白色执政官战团的“原体”表彰这名军士。白色执政官是后继战团，而本段所据的叙事时期，帝国内并无公开存活或活动的原体，因此原条目认为，这里可能把“原体”与“战团长”混用了。

**校注：** 该段“没有存活或活跃原体”的旧叙事条件，与本篇已列莱昂、基里曼回归的较新列表不一致，现标明时点。
