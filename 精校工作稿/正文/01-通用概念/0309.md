## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0309-B0003 -->

<!-- A0309-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

This formation takes advantage of the overwhelming firepower and superior protection provided by the Land Raider. Composed of three such vehicles, including the command tank, the Armoured Spearhead adopts a spearhead formation which it uses to penetrate the enemy's forces at their strongest point. Following behind them is a force of lighter Rhinos and Razorbacks, which seek shelter behind the armoured bulk and punishing fire of the Land Raiders. Once a hole has been breached by the Armoured Spearhead, these carriers swarm in its wake to penetrate deeper in the enemy's rear areas.

<!-- /A0309-B0004 -->

<!-- A0309-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

这种编队利用了兰德掠袭者提供的压倒性火力和卓越防护。装甲矛头由包括指挥坦克在内的三种此类载具组成，采用矛头编队，在敌军最强大的地方打穿敌军。紧随其后的是一支由较轻的犀牛和豪猪组成的部队，他们在装甲部队和兰德掠袭者的猛烈火力下寻求庇护。一旦装甲矛头突破了一个洞，这些运输载具就会蜂拥而至，深入敌人的后方。

</details>

**校订译文**

装甲矛头利用兰德掠袭者战车压倒性的火力与坚实防护，由包括指挥车在内的三辆战车组成，以楔形队列强攻敌军防线最坚固之处。轻型的犀牛战车与豪猪战车紧随其后，借前方厚重车体和猛烈火力掩护推进。一旦打开缺口，运输车便蜂拥而入，向敌后纵深穿插。

**校注：** three vehicles 为三辆车，不是三种车型。

<!-- A0309-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

Originally this formation was used solely by the Iron Hands and Imperial Fists Chapters, however its usefulness has seen other Chapters adopt the formation for their own use, making adaptations to its composition. The Black Templars rely heavily on the Land Raider Crusader, while the Doom Legion use the Land Raider Helios for their siege operations.

<!-- /A0309-B0006 -->

<!-- A0309-B0007 -->
<details>

这种编队最初仅见于钢铁之手与帝国之拳，后来因实战效能出众，被其他战团采纳并调整。黑色圣堂尤其倚重十字军型兰德掠袭者战车；末日军团则用赫利俄斯型兰德掠袭者战车执行攻城任务。
