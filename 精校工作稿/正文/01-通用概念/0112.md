## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0112-B0003 -->

<!-- A0112-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

It is the duty of this group to bring the light of the Emperor to countless worlds in the galaxy with the Imperial Creed being taught to these lost planets. Thus, their mission is to spread the wisdom of the Emperor and ultimately expand the Adeptus Ministorum. To meet these needs, the Missionarus Galaxia works in a number of ways with at least one Missionary often being attached with every exploratory vessel in case Human life is encountered. Should they make contact with a lost community, the Missionary works to learn as much about their customs, culture and forms of worship whilst teaching the natives doctrines of the Imperial Creed. This can be a long process as it involves the replacing of the local primary deities with worship of the Emperor and the introduction of Ministorum practices as well as religious ceremonies. The barbaric and less desirable aspects of the native culture such as blood sacrifices are also removed. In addition, these agents of the Missionarus Galaxia are charged with watching for any other undesirable traits such as genetic mutation, Chaos worshippers or xeno influence. If these methods fail, the Missionarius Galaxia is not above using more brutal and forceful methods to change the religion of a world.

<!-- /A0112-B0004 -->

<!-- A0112-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

这个团体的职责是将帝皇之光带到银河系中无数的世界，并向这些失落的行星教导帝国信条。因此，他们的使命是传播帝皇的智慧，并最终扩大国教教会。为了满足这些需求，银河传教士以多种方式工作，每艘探索战舰上通常至少有一名传教士，以防遇到人类生命。如果他们与一个失落的社会接触，传教士会尽可能多地了解他们的习俗、文化和崇拜形式，同时教授当地人帝国信条的教义。这可能是一个漫长的过程，因为它涉及用崇拜帝皇来取代当地的主要神灵，并引入教会和宗教仪式。当地文化中野蛮和不太可取的方面，如血祭，也被删除了。此外，这些银河传教士的特工负责监视任何其他不良特征，如基因突变、混沌崇拜或异型影响。如果这些方法失败了，银河传教士不会放弃使用更残酷和有力的方法来改变世界的宗教。

</details>

**校订译文**

银河传教团负责把帝皇之光带到无数失落世界，向当地居民传授帝国教义，传播帝皇的智慧，最终扩大国教会的影响。为此，传教团采取多种方式，其中一项常见做法是在每艘探索舰船上派驻至少一名传教士，以备发现人类聚居地时开展工作。接触失落社群后，传教士会尽可能了解其习俗、文化和崇拜形式，同时传播帝国教义。改宗往往漫长，因为需要以帝皇崇拜替代当地主要神祇，引入国教会的习俗与仪式，并废除血祭等被教会视为野蛮或不可取的做法。传教团成员还须留意基因变异、混沌崇拜及异形影响等迹象。如果这些手段无效，他们也不排斥以残酷的强制方式改变当地宗教。

<!-- A0112-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

Missionaries often accompany Imperial Guard, Imperial Navy, Black Templar, and Sisters of Battle forces on their expeditions across the Galaxy. In some cases, this process can take generations before it can see fruitrition and the Missionarus Galaxia is satisfied with the state of natives religion. The missionaries also make use of coded prophecies and prepared omens that can be used by their successors to better create a foothold for themselves. Among all the departments of the Adeptus Ministorum, this organisation is the most free and less disciplined. Thus, the men within their ranks tend to value initiative, courage, tolerance and intelligence above simply raw faith along with the inflexible dedication to the words of the Ecclesiarchy.

**校订译文**

传教士经常随帝国卫队、帝国海军、黑色圣堂和战斗修女远征银河。有时，需要几代人的努力，传教才会见效，当地宗教才会改变到令银河传教团满意的程度。传教士还会编织暗含特定信息的预言、预先安排征兆，方便继任者据此建立影响。在国教会各部门中，传教团行动最自由，所受纪律约束也较少。因此，他们比起单纯的虔信及对教会言辞的僵化服从，更看重主动性、勇气、宽容与智慧。

**英文原文**

Through this body is the Adeptus Ministorum able to send preachers and confessors to benight worlds throughout the galaxy. The Ministorum sends agents of the Missionarus Galaxia to distant worlds in order to spread the word of the Ecclesiarch. The men and women of the Missionarius Galaxia are supreme skilled orators and diplomats, and are also highly resourceful and capable combatants in order to survive in barbaric and belligerent cultures. They are also notoriously known to quickly summon the aid of the Sisters of Battle or even the Ordo Hereticus when they suspected evil from the ranks of their flocks.

## History 历史

<!-- /A0112-B0016 -->

<!-- A0112-B0017 -->
**英文原文**

In 645.M39, preachers from the Missionarus Galaxia made contact with the long-isolated Human worlds at Jerulas who they attempted to bring into the Emperor's Light. These worlds had prospered for centuries and refused to be interfered by a regime that existed thousands of light years away. The Missionarus Galaxia were accompanied by the Black Templars Chapter who commenced the Jerulas Crusade when the first missionaries were killed with their vessels destroyed.

<!-- /A0112-B0017 -->

<!-- A0112-B0018 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

645.M39，来自银河传教士的传教士与杰鲁拉斯长期与世隔绝的人类世界取得了联系，他们试图将其带入帝皇之光。这些世界已经繁荣了几个世纪，拒绝被数千光年外存在的政权干涉。银河传教士由黑色圣堂陪同，当第一批传教士被杀，他们的战舰被毁时，他们开始了杰鲁拉斯远征。

</details>

**校订译文**

645.M39，银河传教团接触了杰鲁拉斯一带长期与世隔绝的人类世界，试图使其皈依帝皇。这些世界已繁荣数百年，不愿接受数千光年之外的政权干涉。黑色圣堂战团随传教团一同到来；第一批传教士遭杀害、舰船被摧毁后，战团随即发动杰鲁拉斯远征。

<!-- /A0112-B0018 -->

<!-- A0112-B0019 -->
**英文原文**

The preachers of the Missionaria Galaxia were known to have established a presence on the world of Sophano Secundus which is the reason why the planet became known to the Imperium. In the early 41st millennium, Saint Casophili served as a Missionary in this organisation where he sought to bring the teachings of the Ecclesiarchy to worlds in the southern Segmentum Pacificus. During the 13th Black Crusade, the rise of the heretical Cults of the Voice led to the preachers and missionaries from the Missionarus Galaxia decrying the anti-Imperial teachings of the Voice of the Emperor which spread throughout the region.

众所周知，银河传教士的传教士在索帕诺二号的世界上建立了存在，这就是帝国知道这个行星的原因。在第41个千年早期，圣卡索斐利在这个组织中担任传教士，在那里他试图将教会的教义带到太平星域南部的世界。在第十三次黑色远征期间，异端教派声音的兴起导致银河传教士的传道士和传教士谴责帝皇之声的反帝国教义，这些教义在整个地区传播开来。

</details>

**校订译文**

索帕诺二号之所以为帝国所知，是因为银河传教团的传道士曾在当地设立据点。第 41 千年初，圣卡索斐利在传教团中任职，致力于将国教会教义传入太平星域南部的世界。第十三次黑色远征期间，异端“圣声教派”兴起；随着“帝皇之声”的反帝国教义传遍该地区，传教团的传道士与传教士纷纷出面谴责。
