**英文原文**

The Legiones Auxilia were Imperial military units (primarily Cohorts of the Solar Auxilia, but also including elements of the Legio Cybernetica) dedicated to fighting in support of the Space Marine Legions during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy.

## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0184-B0003 -->

<!-- A0184-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

Although it varied depending on which Legion they fought in support of, in general the role of the auxiliaries was to support their Astartes masters in the field in whichever way the Legions required, whether that was protecting the flanks of a massed battle formation, holding seized ground, providing a garrison for held areas or conducting artillery bombardments.

<!-- /A0184-B0004 -->

<!-- A0184-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

虽然这取决于他们所支持的军团，但一般来说，辅助部队的作用是以军团要求的任何方式在战场上支持他们的阿斯塔特主人，无论是保护大规模战斗编队的侧翼、守住被占领的土地、为被占领的地区提供驻军还是进行炮击。

</details>

**校订译文**

各军团的具体需求不同，但辅助军的总体职责都是按阿斯塔特的要求提供战场支援：保护大规模战斗编队的侧翼、守住夺取的阵地、驻防控制区，或实施炮击。

<!-- /A0184-B0005 -->

<!-- A0184-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

Most Auxilia formations fought based on the doctrines of the Solar Auxilia, at least initially; over time, however, their tactics and methods were often influenced by their patron Legions.

<!-- /A0184-B0006 -->

<!-- A0184-B0007 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

至少在最初，大多数辅助部队都是根据太阳辅助军的教义作战的；然而，随着时间的推移，他们的战术和方法经常受到他们所辅助的军团的影响。

</details>

**校订译文**

至少在最初，多数辅助军沿用太阳辅助军的作战条令；随着时间推移，其战术与行动方式往往受到所支援军团的影响。
