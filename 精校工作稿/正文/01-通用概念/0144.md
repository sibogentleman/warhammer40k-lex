**英文原文**

The Sect Missionarius Mechanicus are unusual Adeptus Mechanicus organizations, that convert Imperial citizens into the worship of the Omnissiah and lead them into joining the Mechanicus.

**英文原文**

They believe that the worship of the Omnissiah must be increased and that by doing so, the awareness of the Great Work will spread. This Great Work entails that the only route for Mankind to reach salvation is by improving the mind and body through the acquisition of knowledge the Omnissiah provides. Members of the Missionarius truly believe it is their duty to help their fellow Humans avoid damnation and they work to bring non-believers into the glorious light of technology. However as the Adeptus Mechanicus is self-contained, it does not believe in the Sect Missionarius' work of bringing outsiders into the Omnissiah's fold. As a result of this, the Missionarius' converts tend not to be welcomed or embraced by most members of the Mechanicus.

<!-- /A0144-B0003 -->

<!-- A0144-B0004 -->

他们认为，必须加强对欧姆尼赛亚的崇拜，通过这样做，对伟业的认识将得到传播。伟业意味着，人类获得救赎的唯一途径是通过获得欧姆尼赛亚提供的知识来改善身心。传教士的成员真的相信帮助他们的同胞避免诅咒是他们的责任，他们努力将非信徒带入技术的辉光之中。然而，由于机械神教是自给自足的，它不信任传教士教派将外人带入欧姆尼赛亚的牧群。因此，传教士的皈依者往往不受机械教大多数成员的欢迎或拥抱。

**校订译文**

他们认为，扩大欧姆尼赛亚崇拜，才能让更多人理解“伟业”：人类唯有获取欧姆尼赛亚赐予的知识、改进身心，才能获得救赎。这些传教者真心认为，帮助同胞免于永罚是自己的职责，因而努力引导不信者走入技术的荣光。然而，机械修会本身封闭自守，大多不认同吸纳外人皈依的做法，新皈依者也因此常受到冷遇与排斥。
