**英文原文**

The Departmento Colonia is an Imperium organization that deals with establishing Imperial colonies on worlds and also with sending military forces to defend the colonists. If contact is lost with these worlds, then the Departmento Colonia will send out requests to the Imperium's military organizations to check the status of the colonies.
