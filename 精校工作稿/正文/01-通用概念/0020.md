**英文原文**

According to ancient sources, the Men of Gold appeared sometime during the Age of Terra, joining the Emperor as he watched and nurtured humanity. To watch and learn from Mankind, the Golden Race spread across the face of Old Earth, multiplying and establishing Order and Civilization on the anarchy of Nature. Though physically and emotionally superior, the Golden Race eventually created the Men of Stone for the purpose of colonizing the Galaxy. In time, the Men of Stone supplanted the Men of Gold and by M21 their influence had waned. It is ultimately unknown what happened to the Men of Gold.

古老资料记载，金人在泰拉时代的某个时期出现，与帝皇一同观察并培育人类。为了解人类、向其学习，金人遍布古地球，繁衍壮大，并在自然的无序之中建立秩序与文明。虽然在体格与情感层面都更为优越，金人仍创造了石人，以推动银河殖民。此后，石人逐渐取代金人的地位；到第 21 千年，金人的影响力已经衰落。他们最终遭遇了什么，至今无人知晓。
