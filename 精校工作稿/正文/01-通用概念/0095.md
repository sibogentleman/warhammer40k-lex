**英文原文**

The Logos Historica Verita is a new governmental department of Roboute Guilliman's reformed Imperium. Roboute Guilliman awoke in late M41 to find that the Imperium's 10,000 year history was catalogued in an extremely unreliable, secretive, and often contradictory fashion. He created the Logos as a means to rectify this problem and create the first true reliable history of the Imperium.

**英文原文**

The Logos is made up of hundreds of skilled scholars and historians, hand-picked by Guilliman, and chosen for their more liberal views of historiography. The Logos' objectives and policies stand in stark contrast to the Ordo Chronos, which has turned to violence in response to Guilliman's reformations.

<!-- /A0095-B0003 -->

<!-- A0095-B0004 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

历史真理部由数百名技艺精湛的学者和历史学家组成，由基里曼亲自挑选，并因其更自由的史学观点而被选中。历史真理部的目标和政策与时间修会形成鲜明对比，后者因基里曼的改革而转向暴力。

</details>

**校订译文**

历史真理部由数百名学识渊博的学者与历史学家组成，均由基里曼亲自选拔，重视他们较为开放的史学观。该部的目标和政策与时序审判庭截然相反，后者甚至以暴力回应基里曼的改革。
