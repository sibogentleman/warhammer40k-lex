**英文原文**

Those Legion Companies fortunate enough to field squadrons of Sky Seekers, used them as advanced reconnaissance units. They Squadrons could secure a vital advantage on the field of battle, with an ease few other vehicles could match, and were capable of outrunning most foes they were unable to defeat. The Sky Seekers also greatly aided in determining the enemy's most dangerous assets. Once that was done, the Squadrons would direct fire from heavy weapons at the rear of their Legions' advance, to annihilate their targets.
