**英文原文**

A Librarium or Librarius is the command and communications centre of a Space Marine Chapter's fortress-monastery, and the repository for centuries of wisdom and history, culled from the reports, treatises and memoirs of the chapter's greatest warriors and finest minds.

**英文原文**

Most of the servants who work in the Librarium are psykers - those who are full battle-brothers of the chapter are known as Librarians. Librarians hold a functionary rank, describing their role as well as their position. The four battlefield ranks of Librarian, in ascending order of importance, are Lexicanium, Codicier, Epistolary and Chief Librarian. Each of these enters battle ready to support his brothers with a range of psychic attacks and defences.

<!-- /A0243-B0005 -->

<!-- A0243-B0006 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在智库馆工作的大多数仆人都是灵能者 — 那些与战团并肩作战的兄弟被称为智库。智库拥有一个公职级别，描述他们的角色和地位。智库的四个战场级别，按重要性按升序排列依次为编修员、典记员、星语官和首席智库。这些人中的每一个都进入了战斗，准备通过一系列的灵能攻击和防御来支援他的兄弟。

</details>

**校订译文**

在智库馆工作的人多为灵能者，其中具有战团正式战斗兄弟身份者称为智库员。智库员的职级同时表明职责与地位；其四种战场等级由低到高依次为编修员、典记员、星语官和首席智库员。他们随时准备以各类灵能攻防手段，支援战斗兄弟。

**校注：** 本篇英文明确给出由低到高 Lexicanium、Codicier、Epistolary、Chief Librarian；与213分别称Epistolary第三高、Codicier第二高的英文表述不一致，分别保留并标明来源冲突。星语官是本稿Epistolary职衔，与星语者Astropath区分。

In the deepest bowels of a Librarius lie the Chapter archives, where many forbidden and dangerous secrets and lore often are kept. Within certain chapter fortresses, the Librarium serves as the control centre for the base's defence systems.
