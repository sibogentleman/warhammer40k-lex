**英文原文**

Lingua-technis or lingua technis, also known as techna-lingua and lingua technica, is a language of the Cant Mechanicus. The official lanuage of the Adeptus Mechanicus, this binary language has been optimised for quick communication of technical data and servitor commands.

**校订译文**

技术之语（Lingua-technis，亦写作 lingua technis、techna-lingua 或 lingua technica）属于机械之语体系，是机械修会的官方语言。这种二进制语言经过专门优化，用于快速传递技术数据与机仆指令。

**英文原文**

It is a language rarely used outside of the order of Tech-Priests, elements of the Techna-Lingua may be found integrated into the Low Gothic of highly industrialised or technologically oriented cultures. Few outside the Priesthood of Mars can fully comprehend Techna-Lingua, and it is a language replete with internal self-references and allusions to knowledge that remain unknown to those outside the Tech-Priests' closed orders. The Magi of the Adeptus Mechanicus have preserved this form of communication since the founding of their institution in the Dark Age of Technology, and by lore none should speak it forbidden phrases who have not been sanctified by the Machine God.

<!-- /A0129-B0003 -->

<!-- A0129-B0004 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

这是一种很少在技术神甫之外使用的语言，技术之语的元素被发现可能是融入了高度工业化或技术导向文化的低哥特语。在火星祭司之外，很少有人能完全理解技术之语，这是一种充满内部自我参照和知识典故的语言，这些知识对于那些在技术神甫的封闭修会之外的人来说仍然是未知的。自从他们的机构在黑暗技术时代成立以来，机械教的圣贤士一直保留着这种交流形式，根据传说，没有被万机神圣化的人不应该说这种禁忌的短语。

</details>

**校订译文**

技术祭司体系之外很少使用这门语言，但在高度工业化或重视技术的社会，其部分词汇也可能融入当地低哥特语。火星祭司团之外鲜有人能完全理解，因为这种语言充满内部指涉及仅在封闭修会中流传的知识典故。原文称，贤者自机构建立于黑暗科技时代以来便保存着这种交流方式；依教律，未经万机神祝圣者不得说出这些禁语。

**校注：** 原文将机构起点表述为黑暗科技时代，与其他篇章对机械教派正式形成于纷争时代的描述有差别。按本句保留并标注，待核是否将技术传统与组织设立混述。

Like most binary languages, lingua technis consists of a burst of static emitted through the bionic implants of members of the Mechanicum which cannot be understood by unaugmented humans. To the ignorant, voxed lingua technis might sound like little more than a blurted hash of machine noise.
