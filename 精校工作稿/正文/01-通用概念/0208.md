**英文原文**

The Hussars screened the heavy infantry's advance and pursued the foe once the infantry's volleys of boltgun fire set them to flight. They could also be used to mount an assault into the enemy, to buy their Battle Brothers time to take up positions, before retreating back to safety. Only the most steadfast and disciplined of a Legion's assault cadre were assigned as Hussars, though. They were warriors that could be relied upon in the heat of battle and those that excelled were amongst the elite of their Legion. So much so, that many Legionary officers had once served within the ranks of the Hussar Squadrons.

轻骑兵掩护重步兵推进，并在敌人被爆矢齐射击溃后展开追击。他们也可先冲击敌阵，为战斗兄弟进入阵地争取时间，再撤回安全地带。只有军团突击部队中最坚定、最守纪律的战士才能担任轻骑兵；他们在激战中值得信赖，其中的佼佼者更是全军团的精英。许多军团军官都曾在轻骑兵中队服役。
