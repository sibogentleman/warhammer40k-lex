**英文原文**

Locutarus were specialized troops used by the Ultramarines during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. These elite troops were maintained by most Chapters within the Legion in small numbers for use in vanguard and strike operations. Each Locutarus had been selected from Assault Squad veterans.
