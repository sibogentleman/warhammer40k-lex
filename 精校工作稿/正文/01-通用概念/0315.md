**英文原文**

A Skyhammer Orbital Strike Force is a battle configuration used by the Adeptus Astartes in company-level engagements. These formations make use of Drop pods, typically with three squads being deployed, though anything from a whole company to the entire Chapter are capable of participating in the assault. Normally, these Strike Elements deploy between fifteen and thirty Space Marines into combat engagements. Such forces are more than capable of eliminating a portion of the enemy ground defences or decapitating the enemy command structure or even securing a landing zone for a follow-up assault from Thunderhawk Gunships along with more conventional landers.

**校订译文**

天锤轨道打击部队是阿斯塔特修会用于连级作战的一种编组，借助空降舱投入战斗。典型编组为三个小队，通常十五至三十名星际战士；不过，规模也可扩大到整连，乃至整个战团共同发起突击。这些部队足以摧毁一部分敌方地面防御，对敌军指挥体系实施斩首，或控制着陆区，让雷鹰炮艇与常规登陆艇运载的后续部队展开进攻。
