**英文原文**

The Estate Imperium is a division of the Administratum, described as "the million-strong records office of the Administratum". Presumably its purpose is to keep and furnish records of Imperial organizations. The Estate Imperium is also one of the overseers of the vast Departmento Munitorum. It is an ancient organization dating back to the Unification Wars.

<!-- /A0481-B0001 -->

帝国档案部是内政部的分支，号称拥有百万人的档案机关。其职能据推测是保存并提供帝国各组织的记录。它也是庞大军务部的监督机构之一，历史可追溯至统一战争。
