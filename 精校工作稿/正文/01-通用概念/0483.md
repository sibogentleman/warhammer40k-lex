**英文原文**

The Literati are security guards and enforcers of the Administratum. Wearing black sashes and wielding simple firearms, they are uncaring and brutal, maintaining order in the endless lines and petitions of the Administratum's complexes. They also ensure that Administratum employees work their shifts and do not wander off from their designated stations.

**校订译文**

文士是内政部的警卫与执法人员，佩黑色饰带，使用简易枪械，冷漠而残忍。他们在内政部设施中维持漫长队伍与请愿人群的秩序，也监督雇员按时当班，不得擅离指定岗位。

**校注：** Literati 沿现稿“文士”，但本条实际指内政部警卫，不能仅据词面归为文学人员。
