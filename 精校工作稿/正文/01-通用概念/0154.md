The Fabricator-General, also known as the Magos Mechanicus, is the highest ranking position of the Adeptus Mechanicus. A Fabricator-General oversees their respective Forge World along with their deputy, the Fabricator-Locum.

## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0154-B0003 -->

<!-- A0154-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

While many Forge Worlds have their own Fabricator-General, it is the Fabricator-General of the Machine Cult's homeworld of Mars that is the most powerful and prestigious. In addition to overseeing the general Mechanicum, the Fabricator-General of Mars is the head of the Cult Mechanicus and speaker of the Synod of Mars (formerly the Martian Parliament). The Fabricator-General of Mars is most often chosen from the Forge Master of the Olympus Mons Forge Temple.

During the early days of the Great Crusade, the Mechanicum was led by the Martian Parliament. The Fabricator-General acted as the Martian ambassador to Terra and had a seat on the War Council of the Great Crusade. At some point before Kelbor-Hal held the position, the post had evolved into the leader of the Mechanicum.

<!-- A0154-B0011 -->
**英文原文**

At the end of the 41st Millennium the Fabricator-General was Oud Oudia Raskian, but his position was being threatened by Archmagos Belisarius Cawl, the famed creator of the Primaris Space Marines. The Cawl Inferior has been pushing the recently resurrected Lord Commander Roboute Guilliman to make him Fabricator-General, but the Primarch refuses to do so, as the Archmagos is considered a Heretek by much of the Mechanicum and his appointment would led to civil war. Cawl himself insists that he has no interest in a political position, and is unsure why the Cawl Inferior is so persistent in its request.

**英文原文**

Following up on news that the Golden Throne was failing, this new Mechanicus sect expressed doubts as to the divinity of the Emperor as the Omnissiah and wished to take Mars in a new more independent direction from Terra. Qvo stated that Cawl had no interest in such an endeavor and dismissed the representative.

</details>

**校订译文**

乌德·乌迪亚·拉斯基安被推定死亡后，火星圣会代表莉塔·昂特·索贝尔—φ向弗里德里希·阿杜姆·西利普·克沃提出请愿，希望由考尔继任铸造将军，为他们眼中不可避免的帝国内战做好准备。这一新兴机械教派系得知黄金王座正在失效，开始怀疑帝皇是否真为欧姆尼赛亚，希望让火星摆脱泰拉，走向更独立的道路。克沃表示考尔对此毫无兴趣，随后打发走了代表。

**校注：** 英文 to become 的主语省略，结合末句克沃明确表示 Cawl 无兴趣，按请愿拥立考尔理解；并非克沃自行争取职位。
