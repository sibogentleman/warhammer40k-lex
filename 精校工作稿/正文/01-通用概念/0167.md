**英文原文**

Black Sentinels provide security for Astropaths and their facilities across the Sol System and beyond. They are also entrusted with the task of eliminating compromised Psykers, prowling across the gantries and corridors of the Telepathica headquarters on Terra, forever poised for the first twitch or spasm of possession. Well-trained and armed with Lasguns, they are dressed in black armor and wear masks that resemble snarling beasts.
