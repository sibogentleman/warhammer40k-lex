**英文原文**

The Pharos is a pre-Imperial piece of Necron technology that is able to harness empathy to allow the user to find, communicate, or even teleport to a location they either consciously or subconsciously desired, without the use of the Warp. ## History 历史

<!-- /A0585-B0007 -->

<!-- A0585-B0008 -->
**英文原文**

Built from Blackstone, the Pharos array was originally constructed by the Necrons for rapid communications and FTL travel. The Pharos is powered by eight C'tan Shards, one of which is Zarhulash. When the Necrons emerged into their great slumber, the array went inactive. By the Age of the Imperium the only known surviving Pharos was on Sotha. Belisarius Cawl states that he found another Pharos elsewhere, but it was long destroyed. Like most other Necron Blackstone technology, the Pharos is able to negate the effects and presence of the Warp.

**英文原文**

Despite the attempts to dismantle the Pharos, the Necron technology of the Pharos activated its self-repair functions and quietly the Pharos facility rebuilt itself. Millennia later, the formation of the Great Rift reactivated the now fully-repaired and operational Pharos, which was noted by the now-reborn Scythes of the Emperor. The Pharos device itself was destroyed by the combined firepower of an Ultramarines strike cruiser, its escorts and the Cawl's personal ship Zar-Quaesitor before the destabilization could create a black hole.

## Technology 技术

<!-- /A0585-B0020 -->

<!-- A0585-B0021 -->
**英文原文**

A Pharos is a Necron device which allows the Necrons to travel faster than light and communicate over interstellar distances without relying upon the warp. In general terms, it is described as a Quantum Empahtic Resonance Beacon. These beacons, such the one upon Mount Pharos, are believed to each contain an entangled supermass of isolated particles that are perfectly in tune with each other. These devices exploit the fundamental laws of physics to connect multiple parts of reality together. The beacon within Mount Pharos was powered by C'tan Shards. When activated, the device creates a portal to a distant location in space that is keyed to the mind of its user. The beacon within Mount Pharos is only one of hundreds of similar devices (Pharoses), which form a network throughout the galaxy.

<!-- /A0585-B0021 -->

<!-- A0585-B0022 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

灯塔是一种死灵设备，它允许死灵以比光速更快的速度旅行，并在不依赖亚空间的情况下进行星际距离的通信。一般来说，它被描述为量子移情共振信标。这些信标，比如法罗斯山上的信标，据信每个信标都包含一个纠缠超质量的孤立粒子，这些粒子彼此完美协调。这些设备利用物理学的基本定律将现实的多个部分连接在一起。法罗斯山内的灯塔由星神碎片提供动力。激活后，该设备会创建一个通往太空中遥远位置的门户，该门户与使用者的思想相关联。法罗斯山上的灯塔只是数百个类似设备（灯塔群）中的一个，这些设备在整个银河系中形成了一个网络。

</details>

**校订译文**

灯塔使死灵无需依赖亚空间，便可进行超光速航行和星际通信。概括而言，它是一种“量子共情共振信标”。包括法罗斯山上那座在内，每座信标据说都包含一大团彼此纠缠、完全协调的隔离粒子。装置利用基本物理定律，将现实中的多个地点连接起来。法罗斯山的信标以星神碎片供能，启动时会开启一道通往遥远地点的传送门，目的地与使用者的心意相呼应。它只是数百座同类装置之一；这些灯塔共同组成遍布银河的网络。

**校注：** Empahtic为Empathic拼写错误；entangled supermass of isolated particles指大量纠缠粒子构成的整体，并非单个超质量粒子。
