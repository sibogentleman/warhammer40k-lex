**英文原文**

Corvidae Initiates were pilots from the Thousand Sons' Corvidae Cult, during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. They supplemented their aircraft's Targeters with eldritch precognitive senses, that allowed the Corvidae to see into the future.
