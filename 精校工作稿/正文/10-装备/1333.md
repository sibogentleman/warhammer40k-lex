**英文原文**

Vox-Operated is a weapon upgrade that can be applied to any Pistol, Basic, Grenade, or Heavy weapon. This upgrade integrates specifically-coded vox receivers into the weapon’s trigger mechanisms, so that the user can operate it by voice if needed. Users can also operate the shot selector and switch firing modes if applicable merely by speaking the appropriate command. For security reasons each receiver is usually keyed to just one voice pattern. Additionally, if the weapon is equipped with a fire selector, the clip may also be selected simply by speaking aloud.

**校订译文**

声控装置是一种适用于手枪、基本武器、榴弹和重型武器的改装。它将特殊编码的通讯接收器接入扳机机构，使使用者必要时能以语音操作；适用的武器还可用口令操控弹药选择器、切换射击模式。出于安全考虑，接收器通常只识别一名使用者的声纹。如果武器配有供弹选择装置，也可通过语音选择要使用的弹匣。

**校注：** Basic为原规则武器类别，暂译基本武器；不扩成所有基础装备。shot/fire selector依选择弹药的语境处理。
