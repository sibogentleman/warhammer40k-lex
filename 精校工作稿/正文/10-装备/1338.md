<!-- A1338-B0001 -->
**英文原文**

Magos Genetus Iyrzek (attached to the Mechanicus forces of the Achilus Crusade in the Jericho Reach) synthesised Infiltriol, which takes advantage of the strong scent receptors common in most Tyranids. Infiltriol uses essential pheromones extracted from members of the gaunt genus to mask the bio-signature of the subject receiving an application of this substance. Infiltriol is formulated for the chem-receptors of Hive Fleet Dagon only. It is currently completely unknown how it could be created for other Hive Fleets. When a Space Marine's power armour is treated with Infiltriol enamel, most Tyranid organisms do not recognize him as an enemy. This allows him to walk through areas teeming with brood organisms, relatively unimpeded. However, drawing too close to more intelligent and perceptive species such as synapse creatures or vanguard organisms risks revealing the deception and bringing down the Tyranids' wrath upon the intruder.

**校订译文**

基因贤者伊尔泽克隶属于杰里科疆域阿基里斯远征中的机械修会部队。他合成的渗透涂剂，利用了多数泰伦生物嗅觉感受器灵敏的特点：从兵虫类生物提取关键的信息素，掩盖涂用者的生物特征。目前的配方只针对达贡虫巢舰队的化学感受器，如何为其他舰队配制同类涂剂，仍完全未知。星际战士将它涂在动力盔甲上后，多数泰伦生物便不会将他识别为敌人，使其相对自如地穿过虫群密布的地区。但若靠近更聪明、感知更敏锐的突触生物或先锋生物，伪装就可能败露，引来虫群围攻。

**校注：** enamel在此为涂覆装甲的伪装涂剂，不按通常陶瓷工艺称珐琅；原渗透珐琅保留对照。essential是关键的信息素，不是基本分类。

**英文原文**

The wearer must be inside an environmentally sealed unit (such as Astartes power armour) for the enamel to be effective. While bio-masked, a character does not warrant notice by members of the applicable Hive Fleet, remaining effectively "invisible" as long as he does not approach too close to a Tyranid creature. If the wielder is discovered by any Tyranid creature, or any attack on a member of the Hive Fleet, it automatically reveals the wielder to all Tyranids in sight. Naturally, psi-active Tyranids (such as Hive Tyrants) are rarely fooled by such a deception.
