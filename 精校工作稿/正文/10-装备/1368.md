**英文原文**

Frag Assault Launchers are a number of explosive charges attached to the hulls of Land Raider Crusaders and Redeemers. In effect similar to Frag Grenades, they are detonated as the troops within disembark to assault the enemy, hurling shrapnel in all directions.

**校订译文**

破片突击发射器由安装在十字军型和救赎者型兰德掠袭者战车车体上的多枚爆破装药组成。车内部队下车突击时，装药随之起爆，将破片抛向四周，效果类似破片手榴弹。
