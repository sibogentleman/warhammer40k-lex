It was crafted by a long-forgotten Master of the Forge, and consists of an adamantium and electrum carapace, with beautifully engraved runic wards and oaths of eternal vigilance. The fires of war may have scorched the Endurant Protector's surface over and over again, yet the armour has not been found wanting and its current bearer will return to the forge to repair the relic. In doing so, they also temper and strengthen the armour's impenetrable surface in the process.
