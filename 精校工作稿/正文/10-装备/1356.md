**英文原文**

The Carnifex is part of a Space Marine Apothecary's equipment, used to administer "The Emperor's Peace" - i.e. euthanize - wounded Marines whose wounds are too grave to be healed. It is shaped like a pistol, the barrel of which is applied to the wounded Marine's temple. When it is triggered, it sends a metal piston hurtling through the skull and into the Marine's brain, killing him instantly and virtually painlessly.

**校订译文**

刽子手是星际战士药剂师的装备，用来为伤重无法救治的战士施予“帝皇安宁”，即安乐死。它形似手枪，将管口抵在伤员太阳穴后启动，便会推动金属活塞穿过颅骨进入脑部，使其迅速、几乎无痛地死亡。
