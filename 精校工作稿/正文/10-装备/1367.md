**英文原文**

Remote Detonation Packs are another of the many tools given to a Space Marine Scout, as part of their training and are the ultimate tool for mass destruction and ambush. By utilising remote detonation instead of a timer or impact trigger the detonation pack can be planted and then activated at the leisure of the Scout unit, giving them ample time to move to a safe distance.

**校订译文**

遥控炸药包是星际战士侦察兵在训练期间获配的多种工具之一，适合大规模破坏和伏击。它采用遥控引爆，而非定时或碰撞触发，侦察小队可以先安放装药，再自行选择时机引爆，留出充足时间撤至安全距离。

**校注：** at the leisure意为可自行选择时机，不是空闲时才操作；detonation为引爆而非引燃。
