**英文原文**

The Shift Field is a piece of advanced Imperium defensive technology that is being field tested by the Mentors Chapter, and can be carried on the belt or strap of a Space Marine. When activated, the Shift Field creates a protective shield around ten Space Marines, which distorts the light around them and makes it impossible for their opponents to pinpoint them with projectile weapons. Though the Mentors within the Shift Field are able to see and fire out the field as normal, it can not prevent anyone from entering the shield and negating its effects.

**校订译文**

偏转力场是一种由导师战团进行实战测试的先进帝国防护技术，发生器可挂在星际战士的腰带或背带上。启动后，它在约十名星际战士周围形成屏障，扭曲附近光线，使敌人无法用射弹武器准确瞄准。屏障内的导师战团战士仍可正常观察外界、向外射击，但力场无法阻止敌人进入；一旦敌人进入屏障，其保护效果也就失去作用。
