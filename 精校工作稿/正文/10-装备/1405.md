**英文原文**

Developed by Belisarius Cawl, the Vox Espiritum is a powerful neural amplifier that causes its wearer's voxed utterances to resonate on a modulated and heavily warded frequency. Though highly experimental and perhaps dangerous, it allows its user to project their bellowed commands directly into the minds of friend and foe alike.

音阵之魂由贝利萨留斯·考尔研发，是一种强大的神经放大器。它让佩戴者通过音阵发出的言语，在经过调制并施有重重防护的频率上共振。这件装置仍具有高度实验性质，或许相当危险，却能将使用者高声发出的命令直接投射进敌我双方的脑海。
