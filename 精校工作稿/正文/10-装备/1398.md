**英文原文**

Locator Beacons are portable signalling devices that include a teleport homer, broad-spectrum communicators, and geo-positional tracking. They are frequently carried by Space Marine Scout Bike Squads, where they are placed behind enemy lines.

**校订译文**

定位信标是一种便携式信号装置，整合了传送信标、宽频通信器与地理定位追踪功能。星际战士侦察摩托小队经常携带这种装置，并将其部署在敌后。

**校注：** teleport homer沿第1396篇传送信标，不是远程传输主机。
