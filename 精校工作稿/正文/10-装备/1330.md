**英文原文**

A Servo-harness is a special set of tools used by the Master of the Forge of a Space Marine Chapter. It is a mobile shrine to the Omnissiah which consists of many blessed tools, mechanical limbs and weapons. With it, the Master of the Forge can make battlefield repairs of any vehicle, shore up defences, or assist battle-brothers in combat.
