**英文原文**

The Photon Beam Searchlight is a high-powered searchlight used by Space Marines which is able to flash in short bursts, blinding any of their enemies who aren't wearing eye protection. Because of the large amount of power needed to illuminate the Searchlight's magnesium element, only Space Marines wearing Terminator Armour can use them.
