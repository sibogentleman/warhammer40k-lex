**英文原文**

A Tripod is a Weapon Upgrade that can be used on any heavy weapon. The three-legged stand provides a simple, stable platform for bracing a weapon on any relatively flat surface. However, the tripod requires remaining in a stationary position and fixes the weapon in a 180-degree arc of fire.
