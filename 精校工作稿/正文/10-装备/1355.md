**英文原文**

Helix Gauntlets are specialized medical devices worn by Vanguard Space Marine Helix Adepts. Essentially a scaled-down version of an Apothecary's Narthecium, a Helix Gauntlet is capable of retrieving and storing gene-seed from a fallen battle-brother in addition to providing stim-charges that can bring wounded comrades back to fighting strength.

**校订译文**

医疗臂铠是星际战士先锋螺旋医疗员佩戴的专用医疗装置，相当于药剂师医疗套件的缩小版。它既能从阵亡战斗兄弟体内取出并储存基因种子，也能提供刺激剂，使伤员恢复作战能力。

**校注：** Helix Adept沿已定螺旋医疗员，区别正式药剂师；stim-charges不是兴奋弹。
