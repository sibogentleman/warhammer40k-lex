**英文原文**

Extra Armour is used by the Imperial Guard, Space Marines, Daemonhunters, Witch Hunters and Chaos Space Marine forces. Sometimes it is elaborately detailed armour and sometimes simple sheets of ceramite. It reduces the degree to which the vehicle in question is shaken around by weaker impacts.

<!-- A1419-B0003 -->
### Ablative Armour 消融装甲

原题：Ablative Armour 烧蚀装甲

<!-- /A1419-B0003 -->

<!-- A1419-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

A crude and somewhat rudimentary method of enhancing a vehicle’s armoured protection, ablative armour often takes the forms of large sections of plasteel welded or in some cases hooked onto exposed areas of a vehicle’s sides. These types of modifications add to a vehicle’s staying power, but often slow it down due to the excess weight. Ablative armour cannot be repaired and must be replaced.

<!-- /A1419-B0004 -->

<!-- A1419-B0005 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

烧蚀装甲是增强车辆装甲防护的一种粗略而基础的方法，通常采用大块塑钢焊接的形式，或在某些情况下钩在车辆侧面的暴露区域。这些类型的改装增加了车辆的持久力，但由于重量过重，通常会使其减速。烧蚀装甲无法修复，必须更换。

</details>

**校订译文**

消融装甲是增强车辆防护的一种简陋方法，通常将大块塑钢板焊接或挂在车体侧面暴露的部位。这种改装能提高车辆的抗打击能力，却也常因增加重量而降低速度。消融装甲损坏后无法修复，只能更换。

**校注：** Ablative沿本批护甲语境统一消融装甲，原烧蚀装甲作为对照保留在原译。

<!-- A1419-B0006 -->
### Superior Plating 优质甲板

原题：Superior Plating 优质镀层

<!-- /A1419-B0006 -->

<!-- A1419-B0007 -->
**英文原文**

Some vehicles are lucky enough to incorporate rare materials or internal reinforcement within their armour plating, giving them much greater damage resistance than standard armour and certainly more than hastily applied ablative patches. Superior plating often takes longer to repair as regular armour plating.

<!-- /A1419-B0007 -->

<!-- A1419-B0008 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

一些车辆很幸运，在其装甲板中加入了稀有材料或内部加固，使其比标准装甲具有更大的抗损伤性，当然也比匆忙应用的烧蚀装甲要好。与常规装甲相比，优质镀层通常需要更长的时间来修复。

</details>

**校订译文**

一些车辆的装甲板有幸采用稀有材料，或加入内部加强结构，因而比标准装甲更耐损伤，更远胜仓促加装的消融甲片。不过，修复这种优质甲板所需的时间，通常也比普通装甲板更长。
