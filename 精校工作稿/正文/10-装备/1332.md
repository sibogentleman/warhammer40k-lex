**英文原文**

Suspensor Webs were rare and highly sophisticated devices used by Space Marines during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. Attached to portable heavy weapons, they negated much of the typical weight and bulk of these weapons, allowing them to be wielded far more easily. However this effect also reduced the effective range.

**校订译文**

悬带网络是大远征和荷鲁斯之乱期间，星际战士使用的罕见精密装置。将它安装于可携行的重型武器，便能大幅减轻重量和笨重外形带来的操作负担，使武器更易持用；代价是有效射程降低。

**校注：** 抵消笨重程度对操作的影响，并非使武器实体体积缩小。
