## Description 描述

<!-- /A1412-B0003 -->

<!-- A1412-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

Designed to assist in ship boarding, Devastation Drones are armed with an Implosion Charge which inflicts maximum damage within a highly confined area, while reducing the risk of damage to the ships vital systems. They are described as compact, man-sized and built to resist enemy fire. The most advanced units are equipped with anti-grav plates, though the majority rely on heavy treads.

<!-- /A1412-B0004 -->

<!-- A1412-B0005 -->
