**英文原文**

Cluster Mines are frequently carried into combat by Space Marine Scout Bike Squads. Each mine contains a cluster of tiny anti-personnel bomblets, and is triggered by tripwires or pressure sensors. Scouts use these mines to booby-trap the approaches to their position, or vulnerable routes used by the enemy, where they have devastating effects on enemy troops and vehicles alike.

**校订译文**

星际战士侦察摩托小队经常携带集束地雷投入战斗。每枚地雷内有一组小型杀伤人员子炸弹，以绊线或压力感应器触发。侦察兵会用它封锁通往己方阵地的接近路线，或在敌军易受袭的通行路线上设伏，对步兵和载具均可造成严重破坏。
