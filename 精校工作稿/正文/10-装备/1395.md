**英文原文**

Hand-stitched by blinded Servitors and annointed with the blood of a thousand sentries who failed at their posts, this cloak contains strands of mnemothread spun from a thrice-blessed dataloom imbued with data-spirits. It throws up a field of techno-spiritual dissonance that veils its wearer from sight and sensors, allowing them to slip across the battlefield like a ghost.

**校订译文**

这件斗篷由失明的机仆手工缝制，以一千名失职哨兵的鲜血涂抹祝圣。其中织有忆丝，由一台受过三重祝福、寄宿着数据之灵的数据织机纺成。斗篷会形成技术与灵性失谐的力场，遮蔽穿戴者，使其避开肉眼与传感器的侦察，如幽灵般穿行战场。

**校注：** Servitors为机仆，非普通侍从；data-spirits为数据之灵，不擅与Machine Spirit机魂合并。
