**英文原文**

The Fire Wasp is an Adeptus Mechanicus-produced drone used by the Space Marines since the times of the Great Crusade. Usually sent before a boarding action, they are designed to trigger any traps, mines or other surprises laid by the enemy and clear the path to the Marines' main objective.
