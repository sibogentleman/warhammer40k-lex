Horus expected a prolonged struggle against such formidable foes, but the keylekid responded to the battle by first contact by assembling in large pre-exisitng staging grounds all over their domain and awaited the arrival of the Luna Wolves. The keylekid philosophy regarding warfare required them to fight only in these spaces, open-walled roofed structures several kilometres in area.

**校订译文**

荷鲁斯原以为，面对如此强悍的敌人，必然要经历一场旷日持久的战争。然而，凯莱基德在初次接触引发战斗后，却纷纷聚集到领地内各处早已建好的大型集结场所，等待影月苍狼前来。他们的战争观念要求所有战斗只能在这些场所内进行：那是一种占地数平方公里、有顶盖而四面开放的建筑。
