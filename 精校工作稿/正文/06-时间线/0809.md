## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0809-B0019 -->

<!-- A0809-B0020 -->
**英文原文**

Following the eruption of the Eye of Terror Slave Wars, the Emperor's Children raided and destroyed the divided Sons of Horus fortress on Maleum, stealing away the corpse of Horus Lupercal in the process. The Sons of Horus fractured further, and its future seemed bleak. Soon news came that Fabius Bile planned to use Horus' corpse to clone the Primarch, an affront that disgusted all of the former members of the XVIth Legion. Former Justaerin captain Falkus Kibre, then commander of the Duraga Kal Esmejhak warband, knew the only hope was to discover the location of the Vengeful Spirit and with it Ezekyle Abaddon, who had been wandering alone in the Eye for quite some time. Kibre, with the help of the World Eaters Fifteen Fangs warband under Lheorvine Ukris and the Thousand Sons Ka'Sherhan warband under Iskandar Khayon, were able to locate a Word Bearers psychic Sargon who knew the location of the Vengeful Spirit. Sargon was able to guide them to the Eleusinian Veil deep inside the Eye, where the Vengeful Spirit and Abaddon were both waiting. Abaddon revealed that he had sent Sargon out to find his allies, and was already amassing a force to destroy the clone of Horus. Re-assuming command over the Sons of Horus, Abaddon set out for the Daemon World of Harmony, home to the Fortress of the Emperor's Children known as Canticle City.

## Aftermath 余波

<!-- /A0809-B0030 -->

<!-- A0809-B0031 -->
**英文原文**

In the aftermath of the battle, Abaddon emerged as the new Warmaster of the Traitor Legion's and went to work reforging the divided warbands of the Sons of Horus into his new Black Legion, paving the way for the Black Crusades against the Imperium. Meanwhile, the Emperor's Children further descended into fractured hedonistic warbands of slavers. Some Emperor's Children warbands such as the Adored even bent the knee to Abaddon in the direct aftermath of their defeat at Harmony, despite losing many of their comrades in the battle. Fabius' authority over the remaining loyal Emperor's Children was also destroyed by the Battle of Harmony, as they denounced his retreat as a betrayal, while he became increasingly uninterested in leading the dysfunctional remants of his old Legion.

<!-- /A0809-B0031 -->

<!-- A0809-B0032 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

</details>

**校订译文**

战后，阿巴顿成为叛徒军团的新战帅，并着手将荷鲁斯之子四分五裂的战帮重铸为黑色军团，为日后对帝国发动黑色远征奠定基础。帝皇之子则进一步分裂，沦为一支支沉溺享乐、劫掠奴隶的战帮。一些战帮——例如崇拜者——尽管在和谐星一战中损失了许多战友，却仍在战败后立即向阿巴顿臣服。这场战役也摧毁了法比乌斯对仍忠于他的帝皇之子所具有的权威：他们谴责他的撤退是背叛，而他自己也愈发无意领导旧军团那群失去秩序的残部。
