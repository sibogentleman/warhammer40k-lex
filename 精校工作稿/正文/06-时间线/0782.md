**英文原文**

The Crusade of Iron was a campaign of vengeance launched by the remnants of the Legio Praesagius and other loyalist Titan Legions, during the Horus Heresy, following the aftermath of the Battle for Calth.

**英文原文**

The Crusade of Iron was waged in parallel with the Shadow Crusade of the Word Bearers and World Eaters, with the Traitor Titan Legions of the Dark Mechanicum assailing loyalist Mechanicum forces across Ultramar. Other Imperial forces soon flocked to their banner and the Legio and their allies repeatedly clashed with Horus' forces. The Crusade would leave worlds burned by its battles and hideous and terrifying weapons were unleashed, such as the Psi-Titans of the Ordo Sinister. While it raged, the Loyalists fought to preserve the Ultramarian way of life, while the Traitors desired only to leave ruin in their wake.

**校订译文**

钢铁远征与怀言者、吞世者发动的暗影远征同时展开。黑暗机械教麾下的叛军泰坦军团在奥特拉玛各地攻击忠诚派机械教部队，其他帝国部队很快也加入忠诚派的旗帜。泰坦军团及其盟军多次与荷鲁斯的部队交锋，让一个个世界在战火中燃烧。噩兆修会的灵能泰坦等恐怖武器也被投入战场。忠诚派努力守护奥特拉玛的生活方式，叛军则只想留下满目废墟。

<!-- A0782-B0005 -->
**英文原文**

For almost two years the Crusade of Iron raged, attempting to avenge the Betrayal at Ithraca and defend Ultramar from the traitors. Many worlds were the sites of great battles while many more simply burned. During this time scores of Titans met their end and the Legio Infernus felt the wrath of its enemies for the betrayal at Calth. The final battle of the conflict was the Battle of Drooth II, where the campaign's principle leaders, Dae Vergos and Horgoth Nyr, were both slain. In the aftermath of Drooth the loyalists withdrew back to Gantz while the traitors scattered and further fell under the dark influences of Horus.

<!-- /A0782-B0005 -->

将近两年，钢铁远征扩散，试图为伊萨卡的背叛复仇，并保护奥特拉玛免受叛徒的侵害。许多世界都是大战的发生地，而更多的世界只是被烧毁了。在此期间，许多泰坦迎来了他们的结束，地狱火军团因在考斯的背叛而感到敌人的愤怒。冲突的最后一场战役是德鲁斯二号之战，战役的主要领袖达·维戈斯和霍尔戈斯·尼尔都被杀。德鲁斯事件后，忠诚者撤退回甘茨，而叛徒则四散奔逃，进一步落入荷鲁斯的黑暗影响之下。

</details>

**校订译文**

钢铁远征持续了近两年，旨在为伊萨卡的背叛复仇，并保护奥特拉玛免遭叛军蹂躏。许多世界成为大战的战场，更多世界则直接付之一炬。数十台泰坦在此期间毁灭，地狱火军团也因考斯的背叛而承受了敌人的复仇怒火。最后一场战斗是德鲁斯二号之战，战役的两位主要领袖达·维戈斯与霍尔戈斯·尼尔双双战死。战后，忠诚派退回甘茨，叛军则四散而去，更深地沉沦于荷鲁斯的黑暗影响。
