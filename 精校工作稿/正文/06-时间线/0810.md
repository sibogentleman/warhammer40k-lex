## Battle 战斗

<!-- /A0810-B0019 -->

<!-- A0810-B0020 -->
**英文原文**

At some point, prior to the battle, the Captain General of the Custodes presented Guilliman with the Emperor's Sword. Prepared to finally kill his traitorous Brother Fulgrim, who continued to plague the Imperium, the Primarch Guilliman assembled a fleet composed of his Ultramarines Chapter, as well as six of its Successor Chapters and began hunting down the corrupted Primarch. After some time, Guilliman's goal seemed to be close at hand, when his fleet tracked his Brother to the world Xolco. There they clashed with Fulgrim, but the traitor escaped and Guilliman relentlessly pursued him to Thessala and prepared himself to end his Brother's life.

## Conflicting Sources 来源冲突

<!-- /A0810-B0034 -->

<!-- A0810-B0035 -->
**英文原文**

In the 1st edition of Dark Imperium (Novel), Roboute wielded the Gladius Incandor against Fulgrim aboard the Pride. In the 2nd edition of Dark Imperium, he was given the Emperor's Sword to wield against Fulgrim by the Captain-General during The Scouring.

<!-- /A0810-B0035 -->

<!-- A0810-B0036 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

</details>

**校订译文**

在小说《黑暗帝国》第一版中，罗保特在帝皇之傲号上使用赤诚短剑与福格瑞姆交战；第二版则改为：大清洗期间，禁军元帅将帝皇之剑交给他，由他持此剑迎战福格瑞姆。

**校注：** 正文采用《黑暗帝国》第二版的帝皇之剑叙述；第一版的赤诚短剑保留在来源冲突小节，避免将两个版本改成同一武器。
