## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0754-B0003 -->

<!-- A0754-B0004 -->
**英文原文**

Waged after Horus' secret decision to overthrow the Emperor following his experiences on Davin, the war itself seems to have been instigated by Horus after he murdered an Technocracy emissary during negotiations on the Vengeful Spirit. The war was necessary to give Horus the support of the Adeptus Mechanicus for his rebellion, as the Technocracy had possession of multiple STC's the Mechanicum greatly coveted.

<!-- A0754-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

The war was waged primarily by the Sons of Horus of the 63rd Expeditionary Fleet, the World Eaters of the 203rd Expeditionary Fleet, and a contingent of Emperor's Children against the Technocracy's Brotherhood. The war was one of most difficult the Astartes had faced against a non-Imperium human civilisation, thanks to the Technocracy being in possession of two functional STCs. These allowed them to produce power armour and bolters which rivalled that of the Astartes' war gear. While the Brotherhood soldiers were not gene-enhanced like the Astartes, they could produce soldiers, weapons, and power armour in the millions. After 10 months of difficult fighting that Horus used as cover to identify loyalist elements within his forces, the Technocracy was defeated at the Battle of the Iron Citadel. The battle was noted for its brutality, as the World Eaters under Angron slaughtered soldier and civilian alike.

帝国方面的主力是第六十三远征舰队的荷鲁斯之子、第二百零三远征舰队的吞世者，以及一支帝皇之子分遣队；他们共同对抗技术联盟的兄弟会。这是阿斯塔特与非帝国人类文明交战时遇到的最艰难战事之一，因为联盟拥有两套仍能正常运作的 STC，能够生产媲美阿斯塔特装备的动力护甲与爆矢枪。兄弟会士兵虽未经同等基因改造，联盟却能投入数百万兵员，并以相应规模制造武器和护甲。荷鲁斯利用十个月苦战作为掩护，甄别麾下的忠诚派。最终，技术联盟在铁堡之战中败亡；安格隆率吞世者不分士兵平民一律屠杀，使这场战斗以残暴闻名。
