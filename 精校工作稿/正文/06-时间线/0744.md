However, the first major factor that kickstarted the Age of Strife was the sudden appearance of psykers on every human world. This was compounded by massive and persistent Warp storms erupting around the Sol System and other parts of the galaxy. In addition, records report that a great war with the machines began with humanity battling vast robotic armies.
