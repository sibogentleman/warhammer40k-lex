### Third Rangdan Xenocide 第三次冉丹灭绝战争

原题：Third Rangdan Xenocide 第三次冉丹异型灭绝

<!-- /A0760-B0036 -->

<!-- A0760-B0037 -->
**英文原文**

The accounts of the Third Rangdan Xenocide are highly contradictory, and much knowledge about this war was deliberately destroyed by the Imperium. It is known that the conflict took place in the 890s.M30, and generally agreed upon that the Imperial extermination campaign was led by the Dark Angels and Space Wolves under their Primarchs Lion El'Jonson and Leman Russ. The existing sources also agree that the Rangdan had grown powerful once more.

<!-- /A0760-B0037 -->

<!-- A0760-B0038 -->

</details>

**校订译文**

有关第三次冉丹灭绝战争的记载彼此严重矛盾，帝国也有意销毁了大量相关资料。已知这场冲突发生于 890—899.M30 期间；各方通常认为，帝国的灭绝行动由黑暗天使和太空野狼主导，分别受其原体莱昂·艾尔’庄森和黎曼·鲁斯指挥。现存资料也一致承认，冉丹此前已重新壮大。

**校注：** 本篇故意并列互相冲突的史料：第三次战争的 890 年代说、887 年起一年说，以及伤亡与清洗对象的差异均予保留。

<!-- /A0760-B0038 -->

<!-- A0760-B0039 -->
**英文原文**

However, some tellings of the Third Rangdan Xenocide describe it as apocalyptic war during which 50,000 Dark Angels were killed as they fought off a massive Rangdan invasion which brought the northern Imperium to the brink of destruction. These losses eventually contributed to the Dark Angels losing their status as the largest legion to the Ultramarines. Another account claims that the Third Rangdan Xenocide was a decade-long series of catastrophic purges organized by the Dark Angels and Space Wolves to wipe out life on all Rangdan-tainted worlds, including Imperial ones. Accordingly, there were many battles "across vast swathes of the galactic north-west" during the Third Rangdan Xenocide.
