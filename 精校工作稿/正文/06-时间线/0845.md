**英文原文**

The Deliverance of Vulgate was a battle fought by the Grey Knights. It began when a Shrine World Vulgate run by the Ecclesiarchy was overrun by Daemons of Nurgle following the necrophage cults in the catacombs of this planet. The Grey Knights arrived to purge the planet, armed with new Vortimer Pattern Razorbacks. The Grey Knights used these vehicles to launch a series of devastating rapid assaults across the winding catacombs of the world's shrines. The small lightly armed squads were afforded a high degree of mobility and protection by their Razorbacks, and the Psycannons mounted upon each gunned down hordes of Plaguebearers. Upon the successful conclusion of the battle, Grey Knights Master Armourer Vortimer consigned his new variant of Razorback to the Grey Knights Armoury.

**校订译文**

沃尔盖特救援之战是灰骑士参与的一场战斗。国教管理的圣地世界沃尔盖特地下墓穴中出现食尸教派，随后纳垢恶魔席卷了整颗星球。灰骑士携新型沃蒂默型豪猪战车抵达，展开清剿。他们利用这些车辆，在各座圣地蜿蜒的地下墓穴中连续发动猛烈的快速突击。豪猪战车为轻装小队提供了高度机动性与防护，车载灵能炮则击倒成群的瘟疫使。战斗胜利后，灰骑士首席军械师沃蒂默将这一新式豪猪战车改型正式纳入战团军械库。

**校注：** following the necrophage cults 的因果表达略省略，译为当地食尸教派活动之后恶魔侵袭，不额外补写底稿未载的召唤仪式。Master Armourer 依核心首席军械师，非装甲大师。
