**英文原文**

The Spicula Rocket System was a kind of rocket artillery system sometimes mounted on the Space Marine Arquitor Bombard during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. The system consisted of a rack of unguided missiles, fired in rippling salvos at enemy units. It was perfect for taking out massed ranks of infantry and lighter vehicles or building.
