**英文原文**

Rad Weapons were deadly radioactive weapons employed by forces of the Imperium during the Great Crusade and Horus Heresy. Only employed against the most foul Xenos, these weapons detonate with a short, intense burst of radiation and shower the immediate area with deadly radioactive fallout.
