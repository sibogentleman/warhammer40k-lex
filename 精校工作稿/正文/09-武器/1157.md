**英文原文**

The Mortis Machina is a master-crafted power axe, that was forged deep within the subterranean vaults of holy Mars. It has been used by Space Marine Chapter's Masters of the Forge and hews through not just the metal armour of war engines and vehicles, but through their very machine spirit. Even a glancing blow from the Mortis Machina can gut an enemy tank or walker.

**校订译文**

机械之死是一柄精工动力斧，铸造于神圣火星深处的地下秘库。它曾由星际战士战团的铸造大师使用，不仅能劈开战争机械与载具的金属装甲，还能斩伤其中的机魂。哪怕只被斧刃擦中，敌方坦克或步行机甲也可能内部尽毁。

**校注：** master-crafted为精工制作，不是使用者的等级；glancing blow为擦击，而非主动减轻力道的轻击。
