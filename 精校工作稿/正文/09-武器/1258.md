**英文原文**

The Primarch's Wrath is a Space Marine relic. This ancient Boltgun is believed to have come from the personal collection of Roboute Guilliman. Chased in Theldrite moonsilver and inscribed in microscopic lettering with every treatise on tactics that Guilliman ever penned, this weapon's quality is such that it allows the wielder to sweep away great swathes of the enemy with a storm of shells.

**校订译文**

“原体之怒”是星际战士的一件遗物。这支古老的爆矢枪据说出自罗保特·基里曼的私人收藏，饰有塞尔德赖特月银錾刻纹样，并以微观字迹刻下基里曼所著的全部战术论述。其品质卓绝，使用者能够凭借倾泻的弹雨扫灭大片敌军。

**校注：** chased in表示錾刻装饰，不说明整件武器由月银制成。
