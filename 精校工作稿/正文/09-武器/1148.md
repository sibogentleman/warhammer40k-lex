**英文原文**

This weapon was created to the specification of Roboute Guilliman himself after a thorough study of many kinds of power weapon designs across the many planets of humanity. The Legatine axes became a precise and perfectly balanced weapon of war. It is required great skill to master, is swifter to wield than most power axes, but lacks the brute strength of many of its kind. The strongest trait of Legatine axe - skilful blows that inflict maximum harm.
