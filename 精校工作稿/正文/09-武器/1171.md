**英文原文**

The Vinculum Vitae is an incredibly powerful and dangerous artefact Force Sword, that was recovered from the battlefield of Eonis Verge. It was there, that seven Chief Librarians from multiple Chapters gave their lives to banish a horde of Tzeentch Daemons. This left the Vinculum Vitae to be imbued with the terrible, psychic echoes of that engagement, and those who wield it unleash a torrent of catastrophic energy with each blow.

**校订译文**

生命链接是一件极其强大而危险的灵能剑遗物，从伊奥尼斯边区的战场上被寻回。在那里，来自不同战团的七名首席智库员献出生命，放逐了一大群奸奇恶魔。那场战斗留下的可怕灵能回响由此灌注剑中，使持有者每次挥击都能释放毁灭性的能量洪流。

**校注：** artefact是遗物，不是master-crafted精工；武器名称与1167统一生命链接，原别名生命纽带保留对照。
