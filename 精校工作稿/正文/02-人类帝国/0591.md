<!-- A0591-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

During the Dark Age of Technology on a one-way spacecraft Alivia traveled with the man who would become the Emperor to Molech. There, she witnessed the Emperor enter the planet's gateway into the Warp and gain new powers, likely the ability to create the Primarchs. Sureka was kept behind on Molech by the Emperor to watch over the gateway and prevent others from utilizing its abilities. When Molech was garrisoned by the Imperium during the Great Crusade, her role became redundant and she settled down with a single father Jeph and his two daughters, Miska and Vivyen.

<!-- /A0591-B0006 -->

<!-- A0591-B0007 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在黑暗技术时代，阿里维亚乘坐一艘单向宇宙飞船，与后来成为帝皇的人一起前往摩洛。在那里，她目睹了帝皇进入星球的门扉进入亚空间并获得新的力量，可能是制造原体的能力。苏雷卡被帝皇留在摩洛监视大门，防止其他人利用它的能力。在大远征期间，当摩洛被帝国驻军时，她的角色变得多余，她与一位单身父亲杰夫和他的两个女儿米斯卡和维温定居在一起。

</details>

**校订译文**

黑暗科技时代，阿利维亚乘坐一艘无法返航的飞船，与日后成为帝皇的人一同前往摩洛。她目睹帝皇穿过当地通向亚空间的门户，归来时获得新力量，可能包括创造原体的能力。帝皇将她留在摩洛看守门户，防止他人利用。大远征期间，帝国派军驻守后，这项职责已不再需要她承担，她便与单身父亲杰夫及其两个女儿米斯卡、维温一起定居。

**校注：** one-way spacecraft表示单程行程/无返航能力，不是只能向一个空间方向移动。possibly/likely原有不确定性保留。

<!-- A0591-B0008 -->
**英文原文**

Later when Molech was invaded by the forces of Horus during his rebellion, Alivia was caught up in the violence and encountered a group of Ultramarines led by Castor Alcade. Determined to prevent Horus from obtaining the abilities of the Emperor, Sureka convinced the Ultramarines in aiding her to seal the planet's hidden Warp gate. Alivia gave her life trying to permanently seal the gate just as Horus and the Justaerin advanced into its chamber, impaled by Horus' Power Claw seconds before she could complete the ritual. Later however Sureka was apparently revealed to still be alive, appearing before Vivyen and Miska aboard the ship Molech's Enlightenment.

荷鲁斯之乱中，叛军入侵摩洛，阿利维亚卷入战火，遇见卡斯托·阿尔卡德率领的极限战士。为阻止荷鲁斯取得与帝皇相同的力量，她说服他们协助封印隐秘的亚空间门。荷鲁斯与加斯塔林进入门室时，她正试图永久封门；距离仪式完成只差数秒，便被荷鲁斯的动力爪贯穿，付出生命。然而，后来她似乎再次活着现身，在摩洛启蒙号上与维温、米斯卡重逢。

**英文原文**

For the next 2 years Sureka traveled aboard the vessel towards Terra. Just before it entered the Sol System though, the Molech Enlightenment Captain Sulaiman began to argue with Sureka about how they should enter the System. Sureka argued that due to the Warmaster's treachery the Sol System would be heavily defended by the Imperium and its forces would destroy any ship that entered it from the Warp unannounced. She suggested that they should use a less known Mandeville Point, which would give them time to safely contact the Imperium, rather than the well known Elysian Gate, as Sulaiman had planned, which was closer to Terra. The Captain disregarded her suggestion though, both as he viewed her as a civilian, abate one who had become well regarded and called a Saint by most aboard the Destroyer, and he refused to have his ship enter the Sol System like an intruder. He then ordered Molech's Enlightenment to exit the Warp through the Elysian Gate - and in doing so nearly had it destroyed, as the Imperium's forces near the Gate immediately fired upon it.

在接下来的两年里，苏雷卡登上了前往泰拉的船只。然而，就在它进入太阳系之前，摩洛启蒙号船长苏莱曼开始与苏雷卡争论他们应该如何进入太阳系。苏雷卡认为，由于战帅的背叛，太阳系将受到帝国的严密防御，其部队将摧毁任何未经宣布从亚空间进入的船只。她建议他们应该使用一个不太知名的曼德维尔点，这将给他们时间安全地联系帝国，而不是像苏莱曼计划的那样，使用更靠近泰拉的著名的极乐之门。不过，船长无视她的建议，因为他认为她是一名平民，而且在驱逐舰上已备受尊敬，被大多数人称作圣人，他拒绝让他的船像入侵者一样进入太阳系。然后，他命令摩洛启蒙号通过极乐之门离开亚空间，这样做几乎导致摩洛启蒙号被摧毁，因为门附近的帝国军队立即向其开火。

</details>

**校订译文**

此后两年，苏雷卡随舰前往泰拉。即将抵达太阳系时，她与舰长苏莱曼就进入方式争执。她认为，战帅叛变后太阳系必定戒备森严，任何未预先通报、突然跃出的舰船都可能被击毁。因此，她建议从一处较少使用的曼德维尔点进入，争取与帝国安全联络的时间，而非按舰长计划，经距泰拉更近、众所周知的极乐之门抵达。苏莱曼没有听从：尽管舰上多数人敬重她、称她为圣人，他仍只把她看作平民，也不愿让座舰像偷偷闯入者那样进入太阳系。他命摩洛启蒙号从极乐之门跃出，附近帝国军立即开火，几乎将舰船摧毁。

**校注：** abate疑albeit，意思是尽管她备受尊崇，舰长仍视其为平民；不是受到尊敬因此拒绝。

**英文原文**

This forced Sureka to intervene and she asked Sulaiman to evade and keep them alive, long enough for her to ask for help. The confused Captain agreed, not knowing that Sureka was in fact a Perpetual, who had been in psychic contact with one of her brethren, since they neared the Sol System. Known as John Grammaticus, he had repeatedly offered to help Molech's Enlightenment safely reach Terra, if Sureka would tell him where the missing Perpetual Oll Persson was. Sureka had refused to help him before, due to their troubled history together and not knowing where Persson was, but with the Molech's Enlightenment facing certain destruction, she contacted John and agreed to his deal. With her aid secured, John used his influence with the Imperium, to call off the attack on the Molech's Enlightenment, which had been mere moments from being destroyed. The Destroyer was then escorted to Terra by the Battleship Cardinal Boras, where the survivors of Molech safely disembarked. While Sureka and her family viewed Terra's sky, she was confronted by Khalid Hassan, who had been sent to bring the Perpetual to Malcador the Sigillite. However, Sureka refused to go with him, which caused Khalid to tell her he would force her to go if necessary. Before he could make good on his threat though, the grateful survivors of Molech appeared and carried Sureka and her family away from Khalid, oblivious as to what was happening between them. The agent of Malcador could only shake his head and return to his vehicle as the crowd swept passed him, but Sureka knew she had not seen the last of him.

</details>

**校订译文**

苏雷卡只得出面，要求舰长全力规避，为她争取求援时间。困惑的苏莱曼答应了，却不知道她是永生者，接近太阳系以来，一直与同类约翰·格拉马迪库斯保持心灵联络。约翰曾多次提出，只要她告知失踪的欧尔·佩松身在何处，便可助座舰安全抵达泰拉。两人有过不愉快的往来，她也不知道欧尔下落，所以始终拒绝。但眼看舰船必遭毁灭，她终于联系约翰，接受交易。取得她的承诺后，约翰利用在帝国的影响力制止攻击，此时舰船距毁灭已仅差片刻。摩洛启蒙号随后由枢机主教博拉斯号战列舰护送至泰拉，幸存者安全登陆。苏雷卡与家人仰望天空时，哈立德·哈桑出现，要带她去见掌印者马卡多。她拒绝同行，哈立德便威胁必要时动武。尚未行动，心怀感激的摩洛幸存者已涌来，簇拥她和家人离去，根本不知道双方正在争执。哈立德只能摇头，穿过人群回车；苏雷卡却知道，他们还会再见。
