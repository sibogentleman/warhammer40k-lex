**校订译文**

"Horus had called the dark and savage furies latent in the most ruthless, contradictory and ill-starred power of the Immaterium. He had conjured up the fearful idol of an all-devouring Molech of which he was the priest and incarnation. All his powers, hitherto dissipated and scattered, were now concentrated and directed with terrible energy to one terrible aim."— from The Age of Revolution: Suppressed Monographs of Choirmaster Nemo Zhi Meng

“荷鲁斯唤起了亚空间那股最无情、最矛盾、也最不祥的力量中潜藏的黑暗狂怒。他召来了一个吞噬一切的摩洛的可怖神像，而他自己既是祭司，也是这一存在的化身。他此前分散四处、徒然耗散的力量，如今都汇聚起来，以骇人的威势指向同一个可怕的目标。”——摘自《变革时代：星语唱诗长尼莫·志明的查禁专论》

**校注：** conjured up 在此为召来、唤出，不是凭空幻想。Molech 的神像意象沿原文保留，不擅自解说为同名行星。

## Biography 传记

<!-- /A0619-B0004 -->

<!-- A0619-B0005 -->
**英文原文**

A powerful Psyker, he was romantically involved with Aniq Sarashina, a mistress of the Scholastica Psykana. He assigned Athena Diyos to attempt and repair the mind of Astropath Kai Zulane. After the Drop Site Massacre, he dispatched the Master of the Cryptaesthesians Evander Gregoras to purge the City of Sight of Astropaths afflicted by madness when Magnus the Red psychically breached the Imperial Palace to speak to the Emperor. Later when he discovered that Kai Zulane held information vital to the future of the Heresy, he sent Yasu Nagasena on a hunt for the Astropath. After the suicide of Gregoras, Nemo picked up his last journal and began to read what had driven him to it.

<!-- /A0619-B0005 -->

<!-- A0619-B0006 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

作为一名强大的灵能者，他与灵能学院的女教师安尼·萨拉希娜有着浪漫的关系。他指派雅典娜·迪约斯尝试修复星语者凯·祖兰的思维。在登陆点大屠杀之后，当赤红之马格努斯以灵能方式闯入皇宫与帝皇交谈时，他派遣隐感大师埃万德·格雷戈拉斯净化视界之城受疯狂折磨的星语者。后来，当他发现凯·祖兰掌握着对叛乱的未来至关重要的信息时，他派亚苏·那加塞纳去寻找星语者。格雷戈拉斯自杀后，尼莫拿起最后一本日记，开始阅读是什么驱使他这么做的。

</details>

**校订译文**

尼莫是一位强大的灵能者，与灵能学院女导师安尼·萨拉希娜有恋情。他曾派雅典娜·迪约斯尝试修复星语者凯·祖兰受创的心智。登陆点大屠杀之后，红魔马格努斯以灵能强行突破帝国皇宫的防护，试图向帝皇传话，导致视界之城中的许多星语者陷入疯狂。尼莫遂派出隐感者首领埃万德·格雷戈拉斯，清除这些发疯的星语者。后来，他发现凯·祖兰掌握着足以影响叛乱走向的重要信息，又派长濑康追捕他。格雷戈拉斯自杀后，尼莫拿起他的最后一本日记，想弄清是什么促使他走上绝路。

**校注：** 英文把清剿安排在登陆点大屠杀之后，并与马格努斯的灵能闯入相联系；这是底稿时间线，不另凭背景记忆改动。

**英文原文**

Shortly before the Siege of Terra Nemo Zhi-Meng reappeared during a meeting of the Council of Terra. He reappeared during another Council meeting during the early stages of the Siege, where he pointed out the Daemonic nature of many of Horus' allies. In the final stages of the Siege, a shuttle carrying Nemo was shot down and recovered by Katsuhiro and a group of survivors led by Euphrati Keeler who were being guided by a mysterious light away from the Sanctum Imperialis.

**校订译文**

泰拉围城前夕，尼莫·志明出席了泰拉议会会议。围城初期的另一次会议上，他指出荷鲁斯的许多盟友其实具有恶魔本质。围城最后阶段，他乘坐的穿梭机被击落，随后由胜宏及尤弗拉蒂·琪乐率领的一群幸存者找到。这群人正循着一道神秘光芒的指引，离开帝国圣所。

**校注：** recovered by 的行动者是胜宏等人；原文没有说他们击落了穿梭机。

**英文原文**

Alongside Sigismund and his Templars, the group eventually arrived at the Hollow Mountain, where they faced certain death in the face of a massive Death Guard assault led by Typhus. During the battle Nemo Zhi-Meng worked closely alongside Dark Angels Librarians in reactivating the Astronomican, but not to no avail. However Keeler was able to lead a sacrificial prayer amongst the faithful throngs that caused a psychic backlash which not only threw back the Death Guard but also reactivated the Astronomican. The prayer caused many of the faithful to disintegrate into dust.
