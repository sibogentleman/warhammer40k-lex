**英文原文**

Known as the right-hand man of Goge Vandire, Perigno declared the Promethean Cult of the Salamanders as heretical. In the ensuing War of Flames, five companies of the Salamanders were assailed by an Ecclesiarchy army on the world of New Folly. Ultimately Perigno was slain by the Inquisition, which caused his forces to dissipate.

佩里尼奥素有高戈·范迪尔左右手之称。他宣布火蜥蜴战团的普罗米修斯教派为异端，引发了火焰战争。在这场战争中，火蜥蜴的五个连队在新愚星遭到国教军队围攻。最终，佩里尼奥被审判庭杀死，其麾下部队也随之瓦解。
