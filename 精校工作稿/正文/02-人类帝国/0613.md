**英文原文**

Maximal's forges churned out valuable reactors and he was a close ally to Adept Koriel Zeth, who he helped construct the Akashic Reader. Maximal remained loyal to the Emperor in the Schism of Mars, and his forces bore the brunt of the traitor Scrapcode thanks to his utilization of the Noosphere. However Maximal's forces were soon besieged by Kelbor-Hal's twisted Skitarii and Servitors, and while he was able to initially hold out his outnumbered forces were eventually overwhelmed. As his forges were being overrun, Maximal detonated his own reactors in a final act of defiance. Maximal was atomized along with traitor forces in a gigantic explosion.

**校订译文**

马克西姆的铸造厂大规模生产珍贵的反应堆。他与技师科瑞尔·泽斯关系密切，并协助她建造阿卡西克读取器。火星分裂期间，马克西姆忠于帝皇；英文底稿称，他的部队由于使用思维空间而首当其冲，遭到叛军废码袭击。不久，凯尔博·哈尔麾下遭到扭曲的护教军与机仆又将其包围。他起初还能坚守，最终却因寡不敌众而败下阵来。铸造厂即将陷落时，马克西姆引爆自己的反应堆，作出了最后的反抗。巨大的爆炸将他与攻入厂区的叛军一同化为灰烬。
