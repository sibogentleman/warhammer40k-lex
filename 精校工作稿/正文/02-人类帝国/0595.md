## History 历史

<!-- /A0595-B0005 -->

<!-- A0595-B0006 -->
**英文原文**

Ezekiel Sedayne was born into the wastelands of Terra in the Age of Strife. As a young man he hungered for knowledge and wished to help people. Ezekiel had heard of the the Master of the Lines, a man who ensured the purity of blood and genes among the peoples of a region that included the Alapi mountains. He was a man of the old sciences that came to the people during recurring festivals, purifying the blood of young couples. This Master of the Lines would become known as the Emperor. A young Sedayne went on a perilous journey into the mountains almost dying to raiders and the elements to find the Master. Ezekiel had sought the knowledge of the old sciences to help people and upon meeting the Master, he was in awe and felt compelled to serve the man.

<!-- /A0595-B0006 -->

赛丹生于纷争时代的泰拉荒原，年轻时渴求知识、希望助人。他听说阿拉皮山脉一带有位“血统大师”，懂得古代科学，定期在节庆时到访，为年轻夫妇净化血统，维持当地人的基因纯净。这位大师后来就是帝皇。为求古科学知识，赛丹跋涉入山，险些死于劫掠者与险恶天候，终于找到对方。初见大师，他便心生敬畏，决意追随。

Sedayne had a favoured location to take breaks from his duties in the Emperor's service. It was a location in the Emperor's Himalazian facilities that had a view overlooking the remnants of the abandoned hive city of Putoo in the burnt out kingdom of Miamar. It is here where, the Emperor would greet Sedayne as Belisarius Cawl due to the powers of the Pharos. Sedayne's own memory of this conversation would become vague.

**英文原文**

Prior to the beginning of the Great Crusade, the Emperor's greatest servants, His scientists, would educate and train the first Space Marine Chief Apothecaries. At this point, Sedayne was an old man, strong but frail, striding about with a cane like a showman. Sedayne would take part in the Space Marine's tutelage alongside his associates as leader of the 22nd science group. The first Space Marine apothecaries were trained in the science, surgery, and Space Marine physiology. Among these first Apothecaries being taught was Fabius Bile, whom Sedayne met only a few times while they educated the Third Legion, but these encounters had a lasting effect on the young and arrogant Fabius.

<!-- /A0595-B0012 -->

<!-- A0595-B0013 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在大远征开始之前，皇帝最伟大的仆人，他的科学家，将教育和培训第一位星际战士首席药剂师。这时，塞丹已经是个老人了，身体强壮但虚弱，像个表演者一样拄着拐杖大步走来走去。塞丹将作为第22个科学小组的负责人，与他的同事一起参加星际战士的训练。第一批星际战士药剂师接受了科学、外科和星际战士生理学方面的培训。在这些第一批被教导的药剂师中，有法比乌斯·拜耳，塞丹在教育第三军团时只见过他几次，但这些遭遇对年轻而傲慢的法比乌斯产生了持久的影响。

</details>

**校订译文**

大远征开始前，帝皇麾下的科学家负责培养首批星际战士首席药剂师。赛丹当时已老，虽仍气势十足，身体却日渐衰弱，常像登台者般拄杖大步走动。他担任第二十二科研组组长，与同事教授科学、外科手术及星际战士生理学。首批学生中便有法比乌斯·拜尔；在教授第三军团时，赛丹仅与他见过几面，却给年轻傲慢的拜尔留下长久影响。

**校注：** Chief Apothecaries复数为首批，非第一位；strong but frail原并置按气势与身体区分以通顺，不添具体疾病。

**英文原文**

Not long after the Horus Heresy concluded, Ezekiel's body was beginning to fail despite extensive Rejuvenat treatments. Seeking to continue the Emperor's work, Sedayne sought to achieve immortality by obtaining the soul-merging and cloning technology of Belisarius Cawl. Ezekiel called Cawl to Terra, where he had his servant and caretaker, Altrix Herminia shoot Cawl's friend Friedisch. Sedayne promised he would heal Friedisch with his rejuvenat treatments, but only if Cawl combined his mind with his. Sedayne was confident that his mind would prevail. Cawl relented, but the unexpected occurred when Cawl's personality emerged dominant. Sedayne's personality remains deeply buried inside Cawl's gestalt mind.

<!-- /A0595-B0014 -->

<!-- A0595-B0015 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

荷鲁斯叛乱结束后不久，尽管接受了广泛的回春手术治疗，以西结的身体开始衰竭。为了继续帝皇的工作，赛丹试图通过获得贝利撒留·考尔的灵魂融合和克隆技术来实现永生。以西结把考尔叫到泰拉，让他的仆人兼看护人阿尔特里克斯·赫米尼亚射杀考尔的朋友弗里迪希。塞丹承诺，他将通过恢复活力的治疗来治愈弗里迪希，但前提是考尔将他的思想与他的精神结合起来。塞丹相信他的想法会占上风。考尔的态度有所缓和，但当考尔的个性占据主导地位时，意想不到的事情发生了。塞丹的个性仍然深深地埋藏在考尔的完全思维中。

</details>

**校订译文**

叛乱结束后不久，尽管大量接受回春治疗，赛丹的身体仍开始衰败。为继续帝皇事业，他试图借考尔的灵魂融合与克隆技术获得永生。他召考尔到泰拉，命仆从兼护理者阿尔特里克斯·赫米尼亚开枪打伤考尔的朋友弗里德里希，再以回春技术救人为条件，逼考尔同意心智融合。赛丹确信自己的人格会占主导。考尔被迫答应，结果却出乎其预料：胜出的是考尔的人格，赛丹的自我则深埋于其复合心智之中。

**校注：** shoot后以疗伤要挟，原“射杀”过早确认死亡；gestalt mind复合心智，不是完全思维；Friedisch沿完整人名弗里德里希词根。
