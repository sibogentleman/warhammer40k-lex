**英文原文**

He was once highly regarded within the Ecclesiarchy and it had seemed possible he would come to dominate the politics of the Church. However Chirastes was also driven to investigate any claims of Heresy against the Imperial Cult. This led to his downfall, when Chirastes learned that such heretical beliefs were being practiced on the Space Wolves Chapter's Homeworld, Fenris. He began investigating the matter and though it took a lifetime, Chirastes was able to gather enough evidence to initiate an investigation on the world. As the Ecclesiarchy's protocols dictated, he led a delegate-fleet to Fenris, but the servants he sent to investigate the Space Wolves were killed by the Chapter. If the Space Wolves thought this would dissuade Chirastes from his cause they were mistaken. It only made him more driven to find proof of their Heresy and he later returned with a large fleet composed of his own warships and those of three Orders of the Adepta Sororitas. The fleet then attacked Fenris and invaded with its armies to purge the world. However they were beaten back and after three weeks, Chirastes and his commanders knew they were defeated. Chirastes would not retreat, though, until he had gathered the names of every Space Wolf battlefield commander and squad leader who had opposed him. Using a Sensor-vessel, he scanned into their comms and secured a list of names from the battle-cant the Chapter was using. With that complete, Chirastes vowed they would return to gain revenge against the Space Wolves and then ordered his ravaged fleet to retreat.

铁锤小队在基地毁灭前成功逃离，随后追问阿维莉娜名单上最后一个目标的姓名。他们不相信奇拉斯特斯已经完成复仇，希望及时救下那名太空野狼。临终的阿维莉娜终于松口：她已对自己的一生深感怨恨，忠于奇拉斯特斯，结果只是为他的复仇白白耗尽生命。她用最后一口气说出那个名字——“黑鬃·雷拳”。小队从未听说过任何太空野狼叫这个名字，因而怀疑奇拉斯特斯译错了战团的战语。若果真如此，他的猎手追杀的便很可能既有芬里斯事件的参与者，也有与此事无关的人。“雷拳”让他们想到狼主贝雷克·雷拳，他曾因在芬里斯事件中保卫母星而闻名，但他早已去世。小队因此推断，猎手可能把他的继任者拉格纳·黑鬃当成了目标，误将两人的名字拼在了一起。知道拉格纳正在卡迪亚作战，他们立即赶去援救。小队及时抵达，正赶上支点猎手发起攻击。铁锤小队与狼主合力杀死猎手，挫败了奇拉斯特斯最后的复仇企图。
