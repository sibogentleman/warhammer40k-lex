**英文原文**

Monarchia, also known as the Perfect City, was the capital city of the planet Khur. It was brought into compliance by the Word Bearers Legion 103 years prior to the events of Isstvan V.

**英文原文**

It was utterly destroyed by the Ultramarines Legion six decades after being brought into compliance, as an example to both Lorgar and the Word Bearers demonstrating that their worship of the Emperor and the spread of such doctrine could not be tolerated. In the ashes of the city the entire XVII Legion gathered to be reprimanded by the Emperor, Roboute Guilliman and Malcador the Sigillite.

归顺六十年后，极限战士军团将这座城市彻底摧毁，以此警告珞珈和怀言者：崇拜帝皇并传播这种信仰，是绝不容许的行为。整个第十七军团聚集在城市废墟中，接受帝皇、罗保特·基里曼和掌印者马卡多的斥责。
