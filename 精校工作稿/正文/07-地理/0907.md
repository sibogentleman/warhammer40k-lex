## History 历史

<!-- /A0907-B0008 -->

<!-- A0907-B0009 -->
**英文原文**

The Vindicator was developed in 356-372.M31 on Rothern I for heavy siege weaponry in the twisting streets of Stahlenberg, Techmarines of the Ultramarines Legion mounted modified macro-cannon breech and barrel assemblies inside structurally reinforced Rhino APCs. These early Vindicators proved highly effective in the dense fighting conditions in which they were deployed, as they carried far greater firepower than could be mustered by Heretic forces within the confines of the city. The Vindicators were instrumental in the recapture of Stahlenberg and hence ultimate victory in the pacification of Rothern I as a whole.

**校订译文**

356—372.M31年，极限战士军团为在罗瑟恩一号斯塔伦伯格曲折的街巷中使用重型攻城武器，研制了维护者战车。科技战士改装宏炮的炮尾与炮管组件，将其装入结构加固的犀牛装甲运兵车。这些早期维护者在拥挤的城市战场上表现出色：异端军队在有限街区内所能部署的火力，远远不及它们。维护者战车为夺回斯塔伦伯格立下重要功劳，也帮助帝国最终平定整颗罗瑟恩一号。

**校注：** Vindicator依官方维护者战车；Techmarine依官方科技战士，不译技术军士。macro-cannon为宏炮，未泛化成任意大口径火炮。
