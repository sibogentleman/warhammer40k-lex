## History 历史

<!-- /A0977-B0010 -->

<!-- A0977-B0011 -->
**英文原文**

During the 2nd siege of the world in the Great Crusade, the early IXth Legion's 14th Company was deployed under Rogal Dorn. During the campaign they consumed prisoners of war in front of the enemy walls, inspiring terror and fear. They were formally censured after supposedly consuming wounded members of the allied Malagant Rifles as they retreated.

<!-- /A0977-B0011 -->

<!-- A0977-B0012 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在大远征第二次围攻世界期间，早期第九军团的第14连队被部署在罗格·多恩手下。在战役中，他们在敌人的城墙前吞噬了战俘，引发了恐怖和恐惧。据称，他们在撤退时吃掉了盟友马拉甘特步枪兵的伤员，因此受到了正式谴责。

</details>

**校订译文**

大远征期间，在第二次围攻雅兰特三号时，早期第九军团的第14连受罗格·多恩指挥。他们在敌人城墙前吞食战俘，引发极大的恐惧。据称，他们还在撤退时吃掉了盟军马拉甘特步枪团的伤员，并因此受到正式谴责。

<!-- /A0977-B0012 -->

<!-- A0977-B0013 -->
**英文原文**

During the Horus Heresy, Yarant III became the site of a battle between the Space Wolves and a coalition of traitor forces led by Abaddon. The Space Wolves were routed and Leman Russ badly injured, though in the end they were saved by the Raven Guard led by Corax. Later during the Scouring, Traitor forces fled to Yarant after the Second Battle of Vanaheim.

<!-- /A0977-B0013 -->

<!-- A0977-B0014 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

在荷鲁斯叛乱期间，雅兰特三号成为太空野狼和阿巴顿领导的叛徒联盟之间战斗的地点。太空野狼被击溃，黎曼·罗斯受重伤，但最终被科拉克斯领导的暗鸦守卫救出。在第二次瓦纳海姆之战后，在大清洗期间，叛徒部队逃往雅兰特。

</details>

**校订译文**

荷鲁斯之乱期间，太空野狼在雅兰特三号与阿巴顿率领的叛军联军交战。太空野狼溃败，黎曼·鲁斯身受重伤，最后由科拉克斯率领的暗鸦守卫救出。后来，在大清洗期间，叛军又于第二次瓦纳海姆之战后逃往雅兰特。

**英文原文**

The Eldar fought Chaos Space Marines within the Yarant System, with Farseers identifying a site within the Timan valley's Sekarra jungles and swamps on Yarant IV as the headquarters of the Death Guard Chaos resistance. The Eldar showed a diversionary force while Windriders and six Revenant Titans moved in secret toward the valley headquarters - overruning the Plague Marines before they had chance to form an effective defence. Within the week, Chaos forces left Yarant IV.
