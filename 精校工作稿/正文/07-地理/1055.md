**英文原文**

Illyrium is a region in the far north of Macragge (also called Illyria or simply "bandit country"). It was the last region of the planet conquered by the central government, resisting all attempts until Roboute Guilliman was dispatched by his foster-father and Consul Konor to pacify it with an expeditionary force.

**校订译文**

伊利里亚姆是马库拉格最北部的一个地区，又称伊利里亚，或俗称“土匪之国”。它是行星上最后被中央政权征服的地区，曾抵抗所有征服行动，直到执政官康诺派养子罗保特·基里曼率远征军前去平定。

**校注：** Illyrium沿前文和核心定译伊利里亚姆，原标题伊利瑞姆作为原题留在对照稿。

<!-- A1055-B0003 -->
**英文原文**

It remained a haven for outcasts and criminals even after this pacification. During the Imperium Secundus period, Konrad Curze used it as a hideout and established a base of support there, resulting in its devastation by the Dark Angels Dreadwing. Even millennia later, during the Plague Wars, Illyrium was a haven of dissent and mutiny, with its Ultramar Auxilia repeatedly rebelling until the reborn primarch arrived and quelled them.

**校订译文**

平定后，伊利里亚姆仍是社会弃民与罪犯的避难所。第二帝国时期，康拉德·科兹藏身于此，建立起支持基础，最终招致黑暗天使恐翼的毁灭性打击。即使数千年后的瘟疫战争中，这里仍是反叛的温床。当地奥特拉玛辅助军屡次兵变，直到复生的原体抵达，才将其镇压。
