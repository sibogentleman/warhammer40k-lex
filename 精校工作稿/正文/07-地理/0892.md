## Background 背景

<!-- /A0892-B0008 -->

<!-- A0892-B0009 -->
**英文原文**

Third planet of nine that orbited a yellow sun, Sixty-Three Nineteen was ruled by a self-appointed "Emperor", who claimed it to be Terra itself. He therefore reasoned that he was the rightful ruler of mankind, and as such he demanded that Horus' forces bow to him instead. After the initial embassies sent from the 63rd Fleet were met with violence, Horus had no choice but to conquer the world by force. With the removal of the false Emperor, the human population of the planet bitterly accepted their new role as citizens of the Imperium. After the main battle General Rakris was assigned as a Lord Governor Elect of Sixty-Three-Nineteen. Some small resistance did continue however, with several cities on the western continent holding out for brief periods and scattered pacification actions required on three other planets in the system. The most serious incident of non-compliance occurred in the Whisperhead Mountains range and lasted for around three months, but this last pocket of resistance was eventually excised by the Luna Wolves' 10th Company.

## The Whisperhead Mountains 低语之首山脉

<!-- /A0892-B0025 -->

<!-- A0892-B0026 -->
**英文原文**

The Whisperhead Mountain range was located in the southern hemisphere and became the last bolt-hole of the forces combating Imperial rule. The general area featured a population more superstitious than that of the northern regions, and organized religion fell away to something different there. The main population centre was called Katheri, composed mainly of longhouse-style dwellings, but the hold-out forces made their stand in a fortress carved out of the rock of a particularly high-peak that was connected to a plateau by natural rock bridges. Inside the mountain-fortress were what appeared to be several fanes of a primitive religion, perhaps connected to a local superstition about a being called Samus. All of these were destroyed by the Luna Wolves.

<!-- /A0892-B0026 -->

<!-- A0892-B0027 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

低语之首山脉位于南半球，成为对抗帝国统治的最后一个据点。普遍地区的人口比北方地区的人口更迷信，那里的有组织宗教走向了不同的方向。主要的人口中心被称为卡瑟里，主要由长屋式住宅组成，但抵抗军在一座由一座特别高山峰的巨石雕刻而成的堡垒中站稳了脚跟，这座堡垒通过天然石桥与高原相连。在这座山上的堡垒里，似乎有几个原始宗教的信徒，他们可能与当地人对一个叫萨姆斯的人的迷信有关。所有这些都被影月苍狼摧毁了。

</details>

**校订译文**

低语之首山脉位于南半球，成为反抗帝国统治者最后的藏身处。当地居民比北方居民更为迷信，原有的有组织宗教在这里让位于另一种信仰形态。主要聚居点名为卡瑟里，以长屋式住宅为主。不过，抵抗部队据守的是一座从高耸山峰岩体中凿出的堡垒，天然石桥将其与一片高原相连。堡垒内部似乎设有数座原始宗教神殿，可能与当地人对名为萨姆斯的存在的迷信有关。这些场所最终都被影月苍狼摧毁。

**校注：** fanes 是神殿，不是信徒；Samus 是未具体界定的存在，不能直接称一个普通人。
