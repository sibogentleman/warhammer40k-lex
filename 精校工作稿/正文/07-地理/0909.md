## Overview 概述

<!-- /A0909-B0012 -->

<!-- A0909-B0013 -->
**英文原文**

Though controlled by the Imperium, Piscina IV is in a state of constant battle with Orks. Each time the Orks seem to be defeated, the Imperial Guard Regiments stationed on the world come under fire at a different place. The Orks tend to shelter in abandoned mine heads and dig themselves in until they attain sufficient numbers to overwhelm the Guard troops sent to cleanse the area. In recent years, the Dark Angels' stronghold on Piscina IV has been visited more and more infrequently by the Tower of Angels, only coming once a generation or so to take aspirants gathered from the barbaric world of Piscina V for further testing and modification.

<!-- /A0909-B0013 -->

<!-- A0909-B0014 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

尽管受到帝国的控制，但皮希纳四号仍处于与欧克不断战斗的状态。每当欧克似乎被击败时，驻扎在世界各地的帝国卫队都会在不同的地方受到攻击。欧克倾向于在废弃的矿井头避难，并自掘，直到他们的人数足以压倒被派去清理该地区的卫队。近年来，天使之塔越来越不频繁地访问黑暗天使在皮希纳四号的堡垒，大约每一代人只来一次，将从野蛮的皮希纳五号世界聚集的有志者带到那里进行进一步的测试和改变。

</details>

**校订译文**

皮希纳四号虽在帝国控制下，却始终与欧克交战。每当欧克似乎被击败，驻防的帝国卫队各团又会在别处遭到袭击。欧克常藏身废弃矿井口，构筑巢穴与阵地，直到聚集足够数量，便冲垮前来清剿的卫队。近些年，天使之塔造访黑暗天使据点的次数越来越少，大约每隔一代人才来一次，接走从原始的皮希纳五号选拔来的候选者，让他们接受进一步考验与改造。

**校注：** dig themselves in 指挖掘工事并据守，不是“自掘”；aspirants 为新兵候选者。

**英文原文**

The keep's virus weapon failsafe had been activated, and was going to destroy all life on the planet. Boreas realized that the only way that they could keep the planet's population safe was to not break the air-tight seals of the keep's doors, therefore containing the virus inside until its eventual end. Nestor, having been charged with keeping the gene-seed safe, sought to leave and was killed by Boreas, using Hephaestus' plasma pistol. The rest of the Space Marines decided that ending their lives before the hunger or thirst of waiting to die pushed them to leave was the better idea, and detonated meltabombs clasped against their chest as they prayed to their Primarch, Lion El'Jonson.
