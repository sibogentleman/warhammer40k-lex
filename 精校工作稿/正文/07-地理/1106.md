**英文原文**

While it was still ruled by the Imperium, Prefectia was a Fortress World known as the gateway to the strategically important Dovar System. The Tau have characterized the capture of Prefectia to be their greatest victory yet, but in truth the Imperium only regards it as a minor loss.
