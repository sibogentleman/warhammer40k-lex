## History 历史

<!-- /A0975-B0014 -->

<!-- A0975-B0015 -->
**英文原文**

When the Fists Exemplar were charged with watching over the Rubicante Flux, a Warp storm that was sporadic in its eruptions and plagued the Abra Sector located on the outskirts of the Segmentum Solar and uncomfortably close to Ancient Terra. To aid the Fists Exemplar with this duty, they were given the Star Fort Alcazar Astra, which had served the Imperial Fists well during the Horus Heresy. With the Fort towed by their ships, the Fleet-based Chapter patrolled the Rubicante Flux for some time until disaster struck. While the Alcazar Astra was being towed to a new location, a sudden massive warp eruption from the Rubicante Flux blew the Star Fort off course and into the gravitational embrace of a nearby star. Due to the heroic efforts of Chapter Master Dantalion and his Captains, the Alcazar Astra was guided towards the star's nearest planet, Eidolica, where it beached itself. Though the Fort was saved from destruction, Dantalion was one of the Fists Exemplar who lost his life in the crash. With the Star Fort forever grounded, the Fists Exemplar adopted Eidolica as their Home World and the Alcazar Astra became their Fortress Monastery. The Chapter adapted well to their new location and served the Imperium well for millennia until the coming of the Ork Warlord known as The Beast. During the War of the Beast, the planet was overrun by Orks.

**校订译文**

典范之拳曾负责监视鲁比坎特通量：这场不时爆发的亚空间风暴侵扰着太阳星域外缘的阿布拉星区，距离古老的泰拉十分接近。为了协助他们履行职责，帝国将星堡星界城堡号授予战团；这座堡垒曾在荷鲁斯之乱期间为帝国之拳立下功劳。此后，这支以舰队为家的战团让舰船拖曳星堡，在鲁比坎特通量一带巡逻，直到灾难降临。一次转移途中，风暴突然剧烈喷发，将星界城堡号抛离轨道，使它落入附近恒星的引力范围。战团长但塔利恩与各连长奋力挽救，将星堡引向距离恒星最近的行星艾多利卡，最终让它迫降在那里。堡垒幸免于毁灭，但但塔利恩和其他一些典范之拳战士在撞击中丧生。星堡从此无法升空，战团便将艾多利卡定为母星，并把星界城堡号作为要塞修道院。他们很好地适应了新环境，为帝国效力数千年，直到欧克战争头目“野兽”出现。野兽战争期间，欧克攻占了这颗行星。

**校注：** 原文 millennia（数千年）与战团成立、野兽战争之间的年代可能存在跨度问题；忠实保留，待核原出版来源。修复 Dantalion 遇难、堡垒迫降及舰基战团转为定居等关系。

## Geography 地理

<!-- /A0975-B0017 -->

<!-- A0975-B0018 -->
**英文原文**

Though habitable, Eidolica is so close to its system's star that the planet's day cycle is inimical to all forms of life, including Space Marines; anything that is exposed to the world's harsh sunlight without shelter is incinerated.
