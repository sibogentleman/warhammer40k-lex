**英文原文**

The Ruinstorm was a warp storm that appeared from 007-011.M31 during the Horus Heresy, engineered by Lorgar to isolate loyalist forces. It was unleashed by Erebus during the Battle of Calth. The Ruinstorm was further grown and perfected by Lorgar due to the misery and bloodshed he unleashed in the Shadow Crusade.

While it was active, the Warp was unnavigable to loyalist forces. Traitor forces could, however, navigate using secret passages known to Lorgar. Hordes of Daemons of the Ruinstorm were also set loose upon the galaxy following the nefarious actions of the Traitor Legions.

**英文原文**

Among the loyalist forces isolated by the Ruinstorm were the Blood Angels Legion, the majority of which was marooned in the Signus Cluster and cut off from communication with Baal. The storm became so severe that Primarch Roboute Guilliman feared Terra lost and created his own auxiliary state, Imperium Secundus, as a safety measure. Only the Pharos allowed the loyalists to sail through the storm and regroup at Macragge.

<!-- /A0853-B0005 -->

<!-- A0853-B0006 -->
<details>
<summary>原译文</summary>

被毁灭风暴隔离的忠诚派部队中有圣血天使军团，其中大多数被困在西格纳斯星群中，与巴尔失去了联系。风暴变得如此严重，以至于原体罗伯特·基里曼担心泰拉会失败，并创建了自己的辅助国家第二帝国作为安全措施。只有灯塔允许忠诚派在风暴中航行，并在马库拉格重新集结。

</details>

**校订译文**

圣血天使是被风暴困住的忠诚军团之一，大部分兵力滞留西格纳斯星群，无法与巴尔联络。风暴愈演愈烈，罗保特·基里曼担心泰拉已经陷落，于是建立第二帝国，作为延续帝国的后备政权。只有借助法罗斯，忠诚派才得以穿越风暴，在马库拉格重新集结。

**校注：** Terra lost 是担心泰拉已陷落，不是担心将来“会失败”；Pharos 为第585篇法罗斯装置。
